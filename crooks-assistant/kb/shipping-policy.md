# Shipping and delivery

<!-- Editing notes: from the Shipping policy published on crooksldn.com, read 7 Sept 2026. -->

This is the CROOKS shipping and delivery policy as published on the website.

## What it costs

- UK shipping is free over thirty pounds, and Tracked 24 is free over eighty pounds.
- Under those thresholds: standard is three pounds, Tracked 24 is four pounds ninety-nine.
- International shipping is calculated at checkout from weight and destination.

## When it ships

- Orders placed before 6pm, Monday to Saturday, go out the same day. After 6pm they go out on
  the next dispatch day. There is no dispatch on Sunday.
- Limited drops and pre-orders ship on the date shown on the product page.

## When it lands

- Once dispatched: UK one to two working days; international seven to fourteen days.
- Tracking is emailed the moment the parcel leaves us.

## Overseas duties

- Import duties charged on arrival are set by the destination country's customs and are not
  included at checkout. Warn customers so there is no surprise on the doorstep.

## Lost or damaged in transit

- That is on us to sort. If the customer messages within 14 days we chase the courier, or send
  a replacement or a refund. A photo helps for damage.

## How to contact us

- Email crooksldn@gmail.com or DM @crooksldn with the order number. We reply within one to two
  working days, Monday to Saturday.
