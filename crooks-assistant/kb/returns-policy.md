# Returns and refunds

Source: the Refund Policy published on crooksldn.com, read from the store on 7 Sept 2026.
Written as answers, because the assistant reads these aloud. Keep it current — if the policy
page changes, change this.

## Changed your mind, or wrong size

- The customer has 14 days from delivery to return or exchange any unworn item with the tags on.
- Return postage is paid by the customer for a change of mind, a wrong size, a swap, or any
  other reason of their own.
- A UK size swap has no fee itself, and we pay the postage sending the new size out.
- Returns go to: Oairo UK Office, Bourne End Business Park, Bourne End, Buckinghamshire, SL8 5AS.

## Faulty or wrong item

- That is on us. If the customer messages within 14 days we refund or replace in full and cover
  the postage both ways.
- A photo helps us fix it fast; ask for one.

## How a return happens

- The customer emails crooksldn@gmail.com or DMs @crooksldn with their order number.
- We reply within one to two working days.
- An approved refund lands on the original payment method within five to seven days.

## Final sale

- Items marked final sale are clearly labelled and cannot be returned.
- None of this affects the customer's statutory rights.
