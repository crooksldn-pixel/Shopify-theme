# 15 — Marcus, 41, RSI flare-up means the mouse stays unplugged — Tab, Enter, Escape or nothing
**Device:** Desktop 1440×900, normal broadband · **Goal:** buy a pair of jeans using only the keyboard, and find out fast whether this shop is even operable that way · **Mood:** Tuesday, 22:00, braced for the usual — half the web is unusable without a mouse, and a site styled as a "police evidence terminal" doesn't sound like the accessible half

### Step 1 — Landed and started tabbing straight into the cookie wall
**Did:** Page loads near-black, monospace, handcuffs logo. A COOKIE CONSENT panel sits across the bottom. Pressed Tab to deal with it.
**Got:** First stop: the Privacy Policy link inside the banner — sensible, the banner is first in the order. Second stop: "Manage preferences" — and the ring vanished. No outline, no glow, nothing; I only knew where I was because I was counting. Third stop: "Accept" — a proper blue glow around it. Pressed Enter. Banner gone — and my focus got dumped back to the top of the page.
**Expected:** Banner first in tab order (tick), a visible ring on every button (one miss), and focus to land somewhere useful after accepting (it didn't — start again from the top).
**Felt:** One invisible button out of four. If I'd wanted to manage preferences I'd have been pressing Enter on faith.
**Next:** continued

### Step 2 — The screen dimmed on its own
**Did:** Nothing — I was mid-banner when it happened.
**Got:** About three seconds in, the whole shop dimmed under a dark veil and a card faded up: red dot, ACCESS BREACH DETECTED, "CRACK THE CUFFS — escape with a private discount", a BEGIN button, small print. One odd detail: the cookie banner stayed fully lit on top of the dimmed crime scene, like it outranked the emergency. There's an × floating top-right of the screen too.
**Expected:** If a dialog takes over my screen, it should take my keyboard with it.
**Felt:** On-brand ambush, fine. Let's see if I can actually reach it.
**Next:** continued (screenshots 15-02b, 15-16-popup-appeared)

### Step 3 — Tried to Tab to the game — and tabbed a ghost shop instead
**Did:** Pressed Tab, aiming for BEGIN or that ×.
**Got:** The focus ring lit up "Skip to content" — behind the veil. Kept going: logo, CATALOGUE, SEARCH, all faint purple rectangles glowing underneath the dimmed page, while the game card sat there untouched in the middle. A dozen presses in, I was highlighting product cards I could barely read through the fog. The dialog never took my focus, never trapped it, and Tab clearly wasn't going to arrive there this decade (I later confirmed: it's stop number 49, and even then you land inside the game's frame, not on the ×). So I did the thing you learn to do: pressed Escape. The veil vanished instantly, clean homepage back.
**Expected:** To be either trapped in the dialog (proper modal) or at least one Tab away from it. Not "your cursor is now wandering a shop you can't see."
**Felt:** This is the one genuinely disorienting moment of the night. A full-screen "modal" that leaves the keyboard behind it isn't a modal, it's a poster stuck over the till. Escape saving me was luck — nothing on screen said it would.
**Next:** hesitated, then continued (screenshots 15-01c — the ring glowing behind the veil, 15-20-popup-closed-escape)

### Step 4 — Opened the menu drawer — and it behaved like it read the spec
**Did:** Tabbed to MENU (seven stops: skip link, logo, CATALOGUE, SEARCH, BAG [0], LIGHT MODE, MENU — every one with a crisp 2px purple ring). Pressed Enter.
**Got:** Drawer slides in from the right and my focus is immediately on its CLOSE button. Tab walks the drawer: SHOP, ALL, NEW, TEES, DENIM, SWEATS, TRACKSUITS, ACCESSORIES, TRACKING, QUESTIONS, TERMS, CONTACT, the CASE:001 game panel, ACCOUNT, BAG — then wraps cleanly back to CLOSE. Sixteen stops, never once leaking into the page behind. Shift+Tab runs it backwards. Pressed Escape: drawer closes and focus is standing on the MENU button that opened it.
**Expected:** Honestly? A drawer that dumps focus on the body and lets Tab escape out the back. Bitter experience.
**Felt:** Someone built this properly. Focus in on open, a real trap, Escape returns to the trigger — I said "huh" out loud, approvingly.
**Next:** continued (screenshots 15-03c, 15-04c, 15-05c)

### Step 5 — Back through the header to the catalogue
**Did:** Focus was already on MENU, so four Shift+Tabs walked me back to CATALOGUE. Enter. On the catalogue page, took the "Skip to content" link this time — Enter — then Tab.
**Got:** The skip link works: next stop was the FLAT/ON MODEL view toggle, header skipped entirely. Then the category filters, then the product cards, each card one big focusable block reading out its whole record — "NO. 03 DENIM BLUE WASH OG JEANS £60.00 AVAILABLE". Ten presses after the skip link I was ringed around the jeans. Enter.
**Expected:** Cards as a mess of tiny nested links. Instead: one card, one stop.
**Felt:** Efficient. The purple ring on those dark cards is genuinely easy to track.
**Next:** continued (screenshot 15-07b)

### Step 6 — The product page: photos on arrow keys, sizes on Space
**Did:** Skip link again, then tabbed down the buy column. First stop was the big photo, labelled "Evidence photographs" — tried ArrowRight on a hunch.
**Got:** The photo flipped to the on-model shot. From the keyboard. Then the thumbnails, then the sizes: five real buttons, XS to XL. Tabbed to M, pressed Space — it locked in, purple, ring still sitting on M, price steady at £60, "IN STOCK" and "Order before 18:00 and it ships today" underneath. SIZE GUIDE button next in line. Two more Tabs to ADD TO BAG. Enter.
**Expected:** Sizes as some styled dropdown or, worse, divs with click handlers. These are buttons that answer to Space like they should.
**Felt:** Choosing a size took four seconds and I never lost sight of the cursor once. This is what the whole web is supposed to feel like.
**Next:** continued (screenshot 15-10)

### Step 7 — Added — and one press of pure darkness on the way to the bag
**Did:** After Enter on ADD TO BAG, a line appeared right under the button: "Added — 1 in bag · View bag". Header now says BAG [1]. Tabbed forward toward that View bag link.
**Got:** First press: the ring disappeared. Completely. I was on something — turned out to be the big "Buy with shop" purple wallet button, which shows no focus at all. Second press: "More payment options", ring back. Third press: "View bag". Enter — cart page.
**Expected:** The confirmation link to be next after the button, and every stop on the way to show itself.
**Felt:** One-press blackout. Recoverable, but that's exactly how keyboard users end up buying via Shop Pay by accident — you press Enter to "see where you are".
**Next:** continued (screenshot 15-11b)

### Step 8 — The bag and the door
**Did:** Tabbed down the cart page: jeans thumbnail, title link, quantity box, plus button (the minus is properly greyed out at quantity 1 and skipped), Remove, a Discount disclosure that opens on Enter.
**Got:** Then one more invisible stop — the payment-terms widget, ring gone for a press — and then "Check out", big and clearly ringed. Out of curiosity I tabbed past it first: three consecutive presses of nothing (Shop Pay, PayPal, Google Pay express buttons — all invisible to focus). Shift+Tabbed back to Check out, pressed Enter. The real checkout loaded — email, delivery, express options. That's my destination; I stopped at the door.
**Expected:** The invisible stops by now, honestly. And a working checkout button.
**Felt:** Four focus blackouts on the two pages where money changes hands, all of them third-party payment furniture. The shop's own controls never lost me once.
**Next:** continued to checkout — done (screenshots 15-13, 15-14b, 15-15)

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Bought — size M Blue Wash OG Jeans, £60, keyboard-only from homepage to the checkout door: drawer opened and closed, size picked with Space, added, cart reached, Enter on Check out. Nothing on the path was impossible; nothing even needed a workaround except one Escape press.
**Total time:** ~12 minutes (a good two of those spent tabbing at a discount popup that never intended to catch me)
**Worst moment:** The Crack the Cuffs overlay dimming the whole shop while my focus ring kept wandering the page underneath it — "I'm tabbing through a shop I can't see." Its × is unreachable by Tab (stop 49 lands you inside the game frame, past the button), so Escape is the only real exit, and the site never tells you that.
**Best moment:** The menu drawer. Focus jumps to CLOSE on open, Tab cycles all sixteen stops and wraps, Shift+Tab reverses, Escape puts me back on the MENU button. Plus the little grace notes: arrow keys flipping the evidence photos, Space locking a size with the ring holding still.
**Would they come back?** Yes. Under the costume this is one of the most keyboard-honest shops I've used all year — consistent visible focus on every theme control, a real skip link, a real trap where a trap belongs. I'd warn a screen-reader friend about the popup, though.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Nothing would have changed *my* outcome — but move keyboard focus into the Crack the Cuffs dialog when it opens (its close button first). Tonight the shop's flashiest feature is effectively mouse-only: a keyboard user can't reasonably reach BEGIN, so we can't even try for the discount we were just teased with.
