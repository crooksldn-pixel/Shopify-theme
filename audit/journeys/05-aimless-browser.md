**Who:** Came for the aesthetic, or for the game. No purchase intent.
**Conditions:** 390 × 844 throttled. Cold browser. Entered on the homepage.
**Recorded:** what they engage with first, and whether the site gives them a reason to come back or follow.

## Verdict

**Would come back — and would probably follow.**

This is the persona the site is best at, by a distance. The board, the catalogue register, the CASE 001 tie-in, the witness statement and the informant register are five genuinely distinctive things, and four of them are *writing* rather than decoration. A conventional Shopify store cannot do any of this.

The single structural weakness: **every return-and-follow hook is below 2.6 viewports** — CASE 001 at 2.6, WITNESS STATEMENT at 3.6, REGISTER AS INFORMANT at 4.6, socials at 5.9. The homepage spends its most valuable real estate — the first screen — on an attract-mode animation that this persona loves and every other persona has to scroll past. That trade is defensible; the fact that the *payoff* content is four screens further down is not.

**Protect:** the canvas board, the catalogue numbering, the WITNESS STATEMENT copy, and the informant register wording. These are the assets. Two of them are currently buried.
