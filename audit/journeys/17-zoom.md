# 17 — Marion, browses everything at 200% because at 100% she can't read a price without leaning into the screen

**Device:** desktop 1440×900 at 200% zoom, home broadband · **Goal:** buy the £50 charcoal crewneck, today, without asking anyone for help · **Mood:** purposeful. She assumes she'll have to work around something — she always does — and she has no patience for having to guess where a control went.

---

### Step 1 — Opened the site and got a wall of cookie text
**Did:** Typed the address in and waited.
**Got:** A black screen with `12 PRODUCTS CURRENTLY ONLINE` along the top, then a panel headed `COOKIE CONSENT` that took the screen from a tenth of the way down to the very bottom — **90% of the window**. The text: "We and our partners, including Shopify, use cookies and other technologies to personalize your experience, show you ads, and perform analytics, and we will not use cookies or other technologies for these purposes unless you accept them. Learn more in our **Privacy Policy**". Three controls: `Manage preferences`, `Accept`, `Decline`. (`audit/screens/17-01-arrive.png`)
**Expected:** A shop. A cookie strip along the bottom at worst.
**Felt:** I couldn't see a single thing I'd come for. Not the logo, not a product, not a price — one paragraph of legal text at double size and that's the whole computer. I know why it's big; it's big because I asked for everything to be big. That doesn't make it less of a door slammed in my face.

## Outcome

**Bought / didn't:** Got the whole way. £50 crewneck, size M, in the bag, checkout page open with the right item and the right total on it. **At 200% zoom on a desktop this store can be shopped end to end, and that is the headline.** I've abandoned better-known shops than this over less.

**Total time:** About twelve minutes, of which a good two were spent scrolling back and forth between my size and the button that buys it, and about thirty seconds staring at a sweatshirt sitting on top of its own name.

**Worst moment:** The bag. *"Well, what have I actually bought?"* — the photograph printed over the product name, and `Size: M` and the price completely buried underneath it. It's the one page whose entire job is to let me check, and it's the one page I can't read. Runner-up: pressing `Accept` on cookies I'd rather have declined, because `Decline` was the one button with its edge hanging off the screen.

**Best moment:** The size table. Five columns, big cells, my row shaded, nothing cut off, and the `CM`/`IN` switch changing the caption as well as the digits. I tried to drag it sideways twice because I've been trained by every other size chart on the internet, and there was nothing to drag to. Very close second: the header — six controls, all of them words, all on one line, `BAG [0]` telling me it was empty without me touching it.

**Would they come back?** Yes. And this is not faint praise: I would come back *specifically* because the product page and the size chart hold up at my settings, which most shops don't. What I'd do differently is check my order on the checkout page rather than the bag page, because the checkout page is the one that can be read.

**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Get the photograph out from under the words in the bag. On the checkout page the picture sits *beside* the name, the size and the price and it's perfectly clear; on the shop's own bag page it sits *on top of* them. Same information, same photo, one of them readable. Everything else on this list I worked around; that one I simply had to accept not knowing.

## For whoever is protecting the 200% behaviour — what held and what didn't

**Held. Do not let these regress.**

- **The header.** Six controls on one line, no wrap, no overlap, no overflow, `MENU` finishing 48px inside the edge. Words instead of icons is worth more at 200% than at any other size.
- **The size row.** All five boxes on one line, 90px of clearance to the right, no scroller, selection shown as a filled block rather than a thin border, and `IN STOCK` in plain words once a size is chosen.
- **Both measurements tables.** Crewneck (`SIZE · CHEST · LENGTH · SHOULDER · SLEEVE`) and denim (`SIZE · WAIST · INSEAM · LEG OPENING`) both end 107px inside the right edge in cm *and* in inches. **The reported "column clipped at the screen edge with no sign it scrolls" did not reproduce at 200% on either product** — nothing is cut off, and the wrapper is set to scroll if it ever needs to. The cm/inch switch rewrites the caption as well as the numbers.
- **No horizontal scrolling anywhere.** Homepage, catalogue, product, bag, checkout: page width never exceeds the window and nothing sticks out past the right edge on any of them.
- **The menu drawer scrolls.** It looks cut off, but the wheel and the Tab key both reach `TRACKING`, `QUESTIONS`, `TERMS`, `CONTACT`, `PLAY CASE:001 NOW`, `ACCOUNT` and `BAG`. Escape closes it and returns to the `MENU` button.
- **Light mode.** Pressing `LIGHT MODE` gives black text on white at full size, the toggle relabels itself `DARK MODE`, and the size table is just as intact. (`audit/screens/17-20-light-pdp.png`, `audit/screens/17-21-light-measurements.png`) For anyone who zooms, a working high-contrast switch in the header is a feature, not a novelty — I only found it after I'd finished shopping, and I'd have used it from the start.
- **The checkout page.** Nothing clipped, no sideways scroll, item name and size legible beside the photo.

**Did not hold.**

1. **The bag page prints the product photo over the product name, size and price.** The image occupies the first 240px of the row; the text column starts 111px inside it. Result: `CHARCOAL` then `LLBLOCK` then `ECK`, and `Size: M` and the line price entirely hidden. (`17-15c-cart-line.png`)
2. **`+` and the delete bin overlap by 40px in the cart quantity control.** Add-one and remove-the-line are touching, and the destructive one is an unlabelled icon.
3. **The cookie panel's `Decline` button is clipped ~27px past the right edge**, while `Accept` sits comfortably inside it — and the panel itself takes 90% of the window with its `Privacy Policy` link 280px below the bottom, reachable only by scrolling inside a panel that shows no scrollbar. (`17-01-arrive.png`)
4. **`CROOKSLDN` breaks between the D and the N** in the homepage headline — `CROOKSLD` / `N` — with an empty half-screen to its left. (`17-03c-hero.png`)
5. **Nothing repeats the buy controls once they scroll away.** The size row and `ADD TO BAG` cannot both be on screen at 200%, and no bar follows down the page at any scroll position — so confirming your size before you press means scrolling up and back down. On this layout the buy panel is roughly one and a half screens tall, which is presumably why it can't stay with you.
6. **The menu drawer gives no scroll cue.** The only hint that six more links exist is `TRACKING` being sliced in half by the bottom edge.
7. **Two full-screen interruptions before the first garment** — the consent panel and then `CROOKSLDN: THE GETAWAY`. At 100% they're overlays; at 200% they are the entire computer, one after the other.
