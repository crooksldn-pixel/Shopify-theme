# 13 — Jay, 26, phone stuck in landscape since he cracked it — auto-rotate is dead and he can't be bothered to fix it
**Device:** 844x390 mobile landscape, decent wifi · **Goal:** finally have a proper look at CROOKS — a mate keeps going on about the baggy jeans — and maybe actually buy a pair · **Mood:** sofa, Tuesday night, half watching telly, holding the phone sideways like always

### Step 1 — Opened the site
**Did:** Typed the site in and waited, phone sideways as ever.
**Got:** The whole screen went dim with a lone × in the top corner and a blank black box floating in the middle. Nothing in it. For a good few seconds it just sat there, black on black. (13-01-arrival.png)
**Expected:** A shop.
**Felt:** Is it broken? I genuinely stared at an empty black rectangle wondering if the page had died on me.
**Next:** hesitated

### Step 2 — The popup finally filled in, and half of it was missing
**Did:** Waited it out, then read: ACCESS BREACH DETECTED — CRACK THE CUFFS — ESCAPE WITH A PRIVATE DISCOUNT — "Lock all 3 tumblers before time runs out. Tap each one to stop it." Went looking for the tumblers. Swiped up on the box. Tapped around the bottom of it.
**Got:** The instruction line was literally sliced in half by the bottom edge of the box, and the actual game — the three tumblers you're meant to tap — was cut off below it. Swiping did nothing. Tapping did nothing. There were two ×s on screen, weirdly. (13-03-popup-loaded.png, 13-09-popup-after-swipe.png)
**Expected:** To play the silly game and get a discount code.
**Felt:** You're dangling a discount at me and I physically cannot reach it. That's not mysterious, that's just cut off.
**Next:** went back — hit the big × and gave up on the discount

### Step 3 — Cookie banner
**Did:** Popup gone, and now a cookie notice was sitting across the entire bottom half of the screen. Tapped Decline.
**Got:** It covered a solid half of my short little screen, but the buttons were all reachable and it cleared after a beat.
**Expected:** Exactly this, every site does it.
**Felt:** Half my screen, but fine, one tap, gone. Normal annoyance, not a fight.
**Next:** continued

### Step 4 — Actually looked at the homepage
**Did:** Scrolled the homepage with my thumb.
**Got:** Ticker plus header eat about the top quarter, but under it: big CROOKSLDN, OWN THE STREETS™, a CATALOGUE button, then filter chips and huge two-up product tiles that fill the sideways screen properly. Nothing cut off, nothing sideways-scrolling. (13-05-home-clear.png, 13-06-home-scroll1.png)
**Expected:** Honestly? Expected the site to fall apart in landscape. Most do.
**Felt:** Alright, this actually works sideways. The evidence-terminal look kind of suits widescreen.
**Next:** continued

### Step 5 — Into the catalogue
**Did:** Tapped CATALOGUE in the header.
**Got:** ALL — 14 ITEMS, FLAT / ON MODEL toggle, category chips, then the grid a screen down. Two big cards per row, product shots look great this wide. (13-11-catalogue-arrival.png, 13-12-catalogue-grid.png)
**Expected:** A cramped single column.
**Felt:** Nice. Browsing this in landscape is genuinely pleasant, the photos are massive.
**Next:** continued

### Step 6 — Found the jeans
**Did:** Scrolled a couple of flicks, spotted BLUE WASH OG JEANS £60, tapped the card.
**Got:** Product page. Breadcrumb says PRODUCT 03 / 14, DENIM, and then... photo. (13-14-pdp-arrival.png)
**Expected:** Price and sizes somewhere near.
**Felt:** Fine so far.
**Next:** continued

### Step 7 — Swam through the photo
**Did:** Scrolled. And scrolled. And scrolled.
**Got:** The product photo is basically as wide as the screen and square, so on a 390px-tall screen it's nearly three full flicks of jeans before any words appear. The header disappears while you scroll too, so it's just denim, edge to edge, for a while.
**Expected:** One flick to the details.
**Felt:** I'm three swipes deep into the back pockets of these jeans and I still don't know the price. In portrait this is probably one photo; sideways it's a wall.
**Next:** continued

### Step 8 — Size and add
**Did:** Reached the info panel: BLUE WASH OG JEANS, £60.00, size row XS–XL all on one line, IN STOCK, SIZE GUIDE, "Order before 18:00 and it ships today". Tapped M, then ADD TO BAG.
**Got:** M went purple, lovely. Then... nothing. Button didn't change, no drawer, no tick, no little bag flying anywhere. And because the header's gone at this scroll depth there's no bag counter in sight either. I waited, then scrolled all the way back to the top to check — BAG [1]. It had worked, silently. (13-19-after-atb.png, 13-22-back-at-top.png)
**Expected:** SOMETHING. A drawer, a toast, the button saying ADDED. Anything.
**Felt:** Did that go in or not? I nearly pressed it a second time — I'd have ended up with two pairs without knowing. Scrolling back up just to check my own shopping worked is daft.
**Next:** continued, slightly annoyed

### Step 9 — The bag, where the layout finally lost it
**Did:** Tapped BAG [1]. Cart page. Read my item. Idly tapped + wondering about getting two pairs, then thought better of £120 and went to knock it back down.
**Got:** The cart row in landscape is a pile-up: the jeans thumbnail sits ON TOP of everything to its right. My item reads "BLUE WASH OG ...ANS" because "JE" is behind the photo. The price reads ".00". And the minus button on the quantity stepper is completely buried under the thumbnail — the row shows "2 +" and a bin, no minus anywhere. I tapped the spot where minus obviously should be and the site flung me onto the product page instead — the tap hit the photo. (13-23-bag-opened.png, 13-30-stepper-in-view.png, 13-31-after-real-minus-tap.png)
**Expected:** Tap minus, back to 1 pair.
**Felt:** Said "oh come ON" out loud. The button I need is hiding behind a picture of the thing I'm trying to buy, and pressing it teleports me out of my own basket.
**Next:** went back

### Step 10 — Nuked it and started over
**Did:** Back button to the cart — still 2 pairs, £120. Only visible way down was the bin, so I binned it.
**Got:** "Your cart is empty." The bin doesn't take one off, it takes everything. So: back to the jeans, three flicks of denim again, M again, silent ADD TO BAG again, scroll up to confirm BAG [1] again, back into the cart. (13-32-after-remove.png)
**Expected:** Some way to go from 2 to 1 that isn't scorched earth.
**Felt:** I just did the entire purchase twice because a button is hiding behind a JPEG. If I wanted these jeans any less I'd have closed the tab right here.
**Next:** continued — stubborn now

### Step 11 — Checkout
**Did:** Free-shipping bar says Tracked 48 is free ✓ (£10 more for free Tracked 24 — no thanks). Scrolled down past Estimated total £60.00 and hit Check out.
**Got:** Straight into the proper checkout — sudden bright white after all that black, like the lights coming on at closing. Express checkout with Shop Pay / PayPal / Google Pay, email field below. All laid out fine in landscape, nothing clipped. (13-27-checkout-page.png)
**Expected:** Checkout.
**Felt:** Blinding, but solid. This end of the shop clearly knows what it's doing. I'd pay with PayPal from here.
**Next:** continued — stopped at the doorstep of payment, it's nearly half ten

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Bought — reached checkout with the M jeans at £60 and would have paid by PayPal. But it took two full round-trips because of the buried minus button, and a less stubborn person taps out at step 9.
**Total time:** ~16 minutes (this should have been 7)
**Worst moment:** Tapping the invisible minus button in the cart and being thrown onto the product page — "oh come ON" — then discovering the only alternative was emptying the whole cart and starting again.
**Best moment:** The catalogue in landscape — huge two-up product shots, filter chips, no horizontal scroll anywhere on the whole site. And the buy panel (title, £60, one-line size row, ADD TO BAG) fits a sideways screen almost perfectly.
**Would they come back?** Yes — the gear's good and nothing was truly broken end-to-end — but he'd hold the phone upright next time out of self-defence, and he never did get that popup discount.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Any visible reaction to ADD TO BAG (drawer, toast, or a bag counter that stays on screen). That's what caused the doubt, the scroll-backs, and — combined with the thumbnail sitting on top of the cart row — the whole double-purchase fiasco.
