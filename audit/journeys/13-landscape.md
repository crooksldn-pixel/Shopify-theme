# 13 — Dom, on the sofa with his phone turned sideways, not really shopping

**Device:** iPhone held landscape, 844×390, ordinary wifi · **Goal:** none, really — killing twenty minutes, might buy a tee if one grabs him · **Mood:** horizontal, half-watching something else, zero patience for effort

*A note on the numbers below: the screen is 390 pixels tall. Every measurement is against that. "One flick" is a normal thumb scroll, about 300 pixels.*

---

### Step 1 — Landed on the homepage with the phone already sideways
**Did:** Opened the site. Didn't turn the phone — I'm lying down, the phone stays sideways.
**Got:** A thin grey line across the top, `12 PRODUCTS CURRENTLY ONLINE`. Under it a handcuffs logo and a row of words: `CATALOGUE  SEARCH  BAG [0]  LIGHT MODE  MENU`. Under that, `> 12 PRODUCTS AVAILABLE TO PURCHASE`, and the start of a big `CROOKSLDN`. Then, about halfway down, a grey slab slid up and ate the bottom half: `COOKIE CONSENT`. (`audit/screens/13-01-arrived.png`)
**Expected:** A shop. Some clothes.
**Felt:** The top strip and the menu row together take 89 of my 390 pixels before anything happens — nearly a quarter of the screen is furniture, permanently. Then the cookie slab covers the bottom 197. That leaves me about 104 pixels of actual website. A quarter of the screen. I could see the word CROOKS and half of a letter D.
**Next:** continued

### Step 2 — Read what the cookie slab had buried
**Did:** Actually looked at what was underneath before tapping anything.
**Got:** The slab starts 193 pixels down a 390-pixel screen — exactly half. Sitting under it: the shop's name, the line `OWN THE STREETS™`, and the purple `CATALOGUE` button, which is the only button on the entire first screen. The panel's own text is Shopify's: "We and our partners, including Shopify, use cookies and other technologies to personalize your experience, show you ads, and perform analytics, and we will not use cookies or other technologies for these purposes unless you accept them. Learn more in our Privacy Policy" with `Manage preferences`, `Accept`, `Decline`.
**Expected:** A cookie bar. A bar. A strip along the bottom.
**Felt:** In portrait this thing takes 40% of the screen. Sideways it takes half, and the half it takes is the half with the shop in it. The one thing I could have tapped to start shopping — CATALOGUE — is underneath it. It isn't a banner at this angle, it's a lid.
**Next:** continued

### Step 3 — Declined

**Got:** Gone.
**Expected:** That.
**Felt:** Fine. Two real buttons, both big enough, Decline isn't hidden behind `Manage preferences`. Credit where it's due — most sites make you work for that.
**Next:** continued

## Outcome

**Bought / didn't:** Didn't — but it got closer than anything usually does with me. The tee is in the bag, at £25, in the right size. What stopped me was the cart: I couldn't read what size I'd ordered because the photo was sitting on the words, and the button next to `+` was the delete button. I'm not putting a card into a page that looks like it's fallen over. If I'd been sitting up with the phone the normal way round I'd probably have paid.

**Total time:** About seven minutes of dead-slow sofa browsing, and roughly forty-five thumb-flicks from landing to the checkout button — twenty-six of them just walking past twelve products.

**Worst moment:** The cart line item. *"That's just broken."* The photo printed over the title, the colour reading `ACK`, the price reading `00`, and the `+` overlapping the bin.

Runner-up, and the one that actually cost time: tapping `M`, scrolling down, and being told `SELECT A SIZE` by a button I couldn't see at the same time as the size I'd just chosen.

**Best moment:** `> Added — 1 in bag  View bag` appearing quietly under my thumb instead of a cart drawer slamming over the screen. On a 390-pixel-tall screen that restraint is worth more than any feature. Close second: the filter row — `ALL  T-SHIRT  DENIM  SWEATS  ACCESSORIES` — fitting on the first screen sideways, in words, no icons to guess at.

**Would they come back?** Yes, and I'd probably turn the phone the right way up first, which tells you the answer. The clothes are good and the price was right there in the end. But sideways this site spends its first screen on a title card, its second on a cookie lid, its third on a popup, and it never once shows me a garment and its price together.

**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Fix the cart line so the photo isn't printed over the words and the `+` isn't touching the bin. I had chosen, I had added, I was one tap from paying, and the page that was supposed to confirm my order was the page that made me stop.

*Runner-up fix, and cheap: when a size is chosen but a colour isn't, make the big button say `SELECT A COLOUR`. The small line above it already does.*
