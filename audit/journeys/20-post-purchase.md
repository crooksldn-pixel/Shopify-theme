# 20 — Maya, repeat customer with a missing parcel and a pair of jeans to send back
**Device:** iPhone-size mobile (390×844), normal wifi · **Goal:** find out where her second order is, then start a return on the jeans from her first order (too tight) · **Mood:** mildly irritated before she even opens the site — it's 9.40pm, she's in bed, and the dispatch email she's sure she got has vanished from her inbox

> **Note for the audit:** no test login was supplied, so everything below is the SIGNED-OUT experience. What a signed-in customer sees behind the tracking page's sign-in wall was **not testable** and is not covered here.

### Step 1 — Opened the homepage and got two things stacked on top of her
**Did:** Went to the site from memory. Before she could read anything: a full-screen "CRACK THE CUFFS — ACCESS BREACH DETECTED" game popup, sitting on top of a cookie consent box that was itself covering the bottom half of the page.
**Got:** Popup over banner over homepage. Tapped the popup's × (the discount game is for new buyers — they already have her money), then Decline on the cookies. Two taps of admin before the shop existed.
**Expected:** To see the homepage.
**Felt:** "I've literally already bought from you. Move." The popup being aimed at getting a first order while she's here about an existing order made it feel like the shop only cares on the way in.
**Next:** continued — (screens: 20-01, 20-02, 20-03)

### Step 2 — Looked for tracking: opened MENU
**Did:** Didn't fancy scrolling a dark homepage hunting for small print. Tapped MENU, top right. (Tap 1)
**Got:** A clean drawer: SHOP section, then — its own row, own divider — **TRACKING**. Also QUESTIONS, TERMS, CONTACT.
**Expected:** To have to dig for it in a footer. She didn't.
**Felt:** Genuinely pleasant surprise. "Oh. There it is." A tracking link given equal billing with the shop itself is not what she expected from a small brand.
**Next:** continued — (screen: 20-04)

### Step 3 — Tapped TRACKING (Tap 2 — homepage to tracking page in 2 taps)
**Did:** Tapped TRACKING in the menu.
**Got:** "CROOKSLDN PROPERTY TRANSFER NETWORK — IDENTIFICATION REQUIRED. Order records are released to the account they were filed under. Sign in to view the chain of custody for your orders." One purple SIGN IN button. Below it: "NO ACCOUNT? THE TRACKING LINK IN YOUR DISPATCH EMAIL OPENS YOUR ORDER WITHOUT ONE."
**Expected:** A box to type her order number into. There isn't one. There is nothing on this page a signed-out person can *do* except sign in — no lookup, no form, no field.
**Felt:** "Right, but I'm here BECAUSE I can't find the dispatch email." The page is well written — it even anticipates her exact situation — but its only answer is "go find the email you just told us you don't have." The in-world copy ("chain of custody") that felt fun when she was buying feels a bit smug when she's locked out.
**Next:** hesitated — (screens: 20-05, 20-06)

### Step 4 — Tried SIGN IN anyway
**Did:** Tapped SIGN IN. She checked out as a guest, but maybe it'll recognise her email.
**Got:** Bounced off the black terminal world onto a white, rounded, generic Shopify page: "Sign in or create an account", Continue with Shop, or email. Nothing that looks like CROOKSLDN except the name at the top.
**Expected:** The store's own login. This looks like a different company — her first instinct was "is this a phishing page?"
**Felt:** "Where am I now?" She wasn't willing to start creating an account at 9.45pm just to see a tracking number, and (audit note) with no test login the signed-in path couldn't be checked anyway. Backed out.
**Next:** went back — (screen: 20-11)

### Step 5 — Checked the FAQ to see how tracking is supposed to work
**Did:** Back on the site, opened MENU → QUESTIONS. Found "HOW DO I TRACK MY ORDER?" under DELIVERY and tapped it open.
**Got:** "Tracking is emailed the moment your parcel is dispatched. **You can also look your order up on the tracking page — no account needed.**" With a link to the tracking page.
**Expected:** Exactly this — an alternative to the lost email.
**Felt:** Relief, briefly. "See — no account needed. I must have missed the lookup box." (She hadn't.)
**Next:** continued — (screens: 20-07, 20-08)

### Step 6 — Followed the FAQ's link and landed back at the same wall
**Did:** Tapped "tracking page" inside the FAQ answer.
**Got:** The identical IDENTIFICATION REQUIRED page. No lookup. The FAQ's promise — "look your order up on the tracking page — no account needed" — is simply not true; the page itself says the opposite ("no account? use your dispatch email").
**Expected:** A lookup form, because the FAQ had just promised her one in writing.
**Felt:** This was the moment the site lost her trust. "You literally just told me I could do this." One page is lying and she can't tell which, which makes her wonder what else on the FAQ is out of date. Also the quiet realisation that there is nothing useful a signed-out customer can do here at all: her practical options are dig through Gmail again, make an account, or email a gmail address and wait 1–2 working days.
**Next:** went back, gave up on tracking — (screen: 20-12)

### Step 7 — Switched goals: start the jeans return. Tapped REFUNDS in the footer
**Did:** Scrolled to the footer looking for a returns link. Options: QUESTIONS, TERMS, SHIPPING, REFUNDS, TRACK ORDER. Nothing says RETURNS, so she tapped REFUNDS — the obvious candidate.
**Got:** The refund policy. Friendly enough, 14 days, size swaps free — but its instruction for actually doing it is "email crooksldn@gmail.com or DM @crooksldn with your order number", plus a postal address. No form, no link to any returns portal.
**Expected:** A "start your return" button.
**Felt:** "So I just… email them? In 2026?" Deflating — and (she finds out two minutes later) wrong: a proper returns portal exists, this page just never mentions it. The one page named after getting your money back doesn't tell you the real way to start.
**Next:** went back — (screens: 20-10, 20-13)

### Step 8 — Found the real returns route in the FAQ
**Did:** Tried QUESTIONS again (footer link), opened "CAN I RETURN SOMETHING?" under RETURNS AND REFUNDS.
**Got:** "Yes. **Start your return here** — it takes your order number and email. You have 14 days from delivery to tell us, and 14 days from then to post it back…" — an actual link, at last.
**Expected:** Another "email us". This was better.
**Felt:** "THERE it is. Why isn't this in the footer?" The answer itself is the clearest paragraph she's read all night — deadline, condition, who pays postage, four lines.
**Next:** continued — (screen: 20-09)

### Step 9 — Tapped through to the returns centre
**Did:** Tapped "Start your return here."
**Got:** An external Returns Center (aftership.com) — and it loads properly: CROOKSLDN handcuffs logo at the top, a card asking Order number + Email, verify by postal code or phone, "Find your order" button, "Back to shop". Also its own giant cookie banner — the second cookie interrogation of the night — squatting over the form until dismissed. She has her order number (it's on the confirmation email she DOES still have), so this form she could actually complete. She stopped at the form — nothing was submitted, and it's 10pm; the jeans can go back tomorrow.
**Expected:** Honestly, after the tracking page, she expected another login wall. Instead this is the one place tonight that asks for things she actually possesses.
**Felt:** "Finally — order number and email, I have those." Mixed with: "so the RETURNS system can find my order with just my email… why can't the TRACKING page?" That contrast is the whole evening in one sentence.
**Next:** continued to the form, done for tonight — (screen: 20-14)

**Tap counts (after clearing the popup + cookie banner):**
- Homepage → tracking page: **2 taps** (MENU → TRACKING). Findability is genuinely good; usefulness signed-out is zero.
- Homepage → returns centre form, clean route: **4 taps** (MENU → QUESTIONS → "CAN I RETURN SOMETHING?" → "Start your return here"). Her actual route was 7 taps because the footer's REFUNDS link is a decoy that never mentions the returns centre.
- The Aftership destination loaded and presented the order-number + email form. Nothing was submitted (read-only audit).

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** n/a — post-purchase visit. Tracking: failed, gave up (signed out there is literally nothing to do). Return: succeeded — reached the returns centre form with everything she needs to finish it tomorrow.
**Total time:** ~13 minutes, most of it spent going in circles between the FAQ and the tracking page.
**Worst moment:** Step 6 — the FAQ promising "look your order up on the tracking page — no account needed", and the tracking page immediately demanding identification. Out loud: "You just told me I didn't need an account. Which one of you is lying?"
**Best moment:** TRACKING as a first-class item in the main menu (2 taps — she's seen big retailers bury it deeper), and the returns centre asking only for order number + email — the one system tonight that wanted something she actually had. The branded handcuffs on the Aftership page was a nice touch of continuity.
**Would they come back?** To shop — probably, the clothes aren't the problem. But she'll checkout with an account next time purely out of self-defence, and if this parcel doesn't turn up by Thursday she's emailing a gmail address with a knot in her stomach. "Looked after" is not how she'd put it: the money-in journey is polished, the money-already-taken journey is one working page (returns) and one locked door with a misleading sign on it (tracking).
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** An order-number + email lookup on the tracking page — exactly what the FAQ already promises and exactly what their own returns centre proves is possible. (Failing that: make the FAQ stop promising it, and put a RETURNS link in the footer where REFUNDS currently dead-ends people.)
