# 04 — Dan, 27, wants the grey crewneck and maybe the matching look, has never met a "complete the set" control in his life
**Device:** iPhone-size mobile (390×844), home wifi, Tuesday ~21:30 · **Goal:** buy the CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK CREWNECK (£50), seen it on the brand's homepage before · **Mood:** decided on the top, open to persuasion, mildly tired

### Step 1 — Opened the site, straight into the theatre
**Did:** Typed the site in, landed on the homepage.
**Got:** A full-screen popup first — "ACCESS BREACH DETECTED / CRACK THE CUFFS / ESCAPE WITH A PRIVATE DISCOUNT", a BEGIN button, small print about codes expiring in 60 minutes. Behind it, a cookie consent box pinned over the bottom. (04-01-arrival-home.png)
**Expected:** The shop.
**Felt:** A discount game before I've seen a single jumper. Any other night I might have played it — "one attempt per customer" actually made me not want to waste my go at 9:30pm when I just want to buy a crewneck. Closed it. Then tapped Accept on the cookies without reading, obviously.
**Next:** continued

### Step 2 — Found the crewneck immediately
**Did:** Scrolled down the homepage into the catalogue grid. (04-03-home-clear.png)
**Got:** "14 PRODUCTS AVAILABLE TO PURCHASE", and the crewneck is literally NO. 01, first tile: CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK CREWNECK £50.00 AVAILABLE. Clocked NO. 02 next to it — CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK SHORTS £45.00. Tapped the crewneck. (04-04-catalogue-crewneck-tile.png)
**Expected:** To have to dig through a collection page.
**Felt:** Easiest find I've ever had. And I noticed the matching shorts sat right next to it — filed that away, £45.
**Next:** continued

### Step 3 — Product page, picked my size
**Did:** Landed on the crewneck page, everything on one screen — photo, £50.00, size row XS–XL, delivery promise. Tapped M. (04-05-pdp-arrival.png, 04-06-picked-M.png)
**Got:** M highlighted purple, IN STOCK, "Order before 18:00 and it ships today (Mon–Sat)" with "Ordered now — leaves tomorrow" underneath. The little bar stuck to the bottom of the screen had said "£50.00 · XS" before I touched anything, which threw me for a second — I never picked XS — but it flipped to M when I did.
**Expected:** Exactly this.
**Felt:** Clean. It's Tuesday night so "leaves tomorrow" is honest rather than hyped, which I rate. The bar claiming XS on its own was a bit presumptuous though.
**Next:** continued

### Step 4 — Met the "complete the set" line, cold
**Did:** Below the stock line there was a thin row: an empty checkbox, a tiny photo of grey shorts, and "Cop the full fit — add the matching Cellblock Shorts. Save £10." Stopped and read it twice. (04-07-set-line-collapsed.png)
**Got:** Just that one line. No price on it. Nothing else until you touch it.
**Expected:** Honestly, nothing — I've never seen a tick-box like this on a product page. Upsells are usually a carousel at the bottom.
**Felt:** I got the idea fast — tick it and the shorts come too, tenner off. The little photo of the shorts did most of the work. But "Save £10" off WHAT? It doesn't say what the shorts cost or what the pair costs, so at this point the offer is a number with no anchor. I remembered the shorts were £45 from the homepage, so I was already doing 50+45 in my head.
**Next:** hesitated

### Step 5 — Checked the maths before trusting it
**Did:** Instead of ticking it, scrolled down to "MORE FROM THIS DROP" to double-check the shorts price. (04-10-maths-check-shorts-tile.png)
**Got:** CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK SHORTS — £45.00, right there on the same page.
**Expected:** To have to open the shorts page in a new tab.
**Felt:** Fine — £50 + £45 = £95, so a tenner off means £85. I did that myself though; the offer line didn't do it for me. I'm the sort who checks. Someone less suspicious just has to take "Save £10" on faith at this point.
**Next:** continued

### Step 6 — Ticked the box
**Did:** Scrolled back up and tapped the "Cop the full fit" row.
**Got:** The box ticked and a panel unfolded: "CELLBLOCK SHORTS SIZE" with its own XS–XL row, then "£95 £85 for the set" with the £95 struck through, then "FREE UK TRACKED 24 INCLUDED". And the big buy button changed itself to "ADD THE FULL FIT — £85". (04-08-set-ticked-open.png)
**Expected:** Maybe a popup, maybe it'd just silently add the shorts. Didn't know.
**Felt:** OK, now it all lands. Crossed-out £95, £85 for the set — that's the maths I did in my head, printed, so I trust the tenner. Separate size row for the shorts made it obvious I'm getting two garments, not some "set" that's secretly one item. The button saying £85 sealed it. This went from vague to completely clear in one tap.
**Next:** continued

### Step 7 — Nearly bought the wrong shorts size
**Did:** Went to pick L for the shorts — and noticed M was ALREADY selected in the shorts row.
**Got:** The shorts size had quietly copied my top size. I hadn't touched that row. Tapped L; it switched, price stayed £85. (04-09-partner-L-picked.png)
**Expected:** An unpicked row that made me choose — or at least something telling me it had picked for me.
**Felt:** Hang on — if I hadn't looked I'd have added medium shorts. I'm M up top and L in bottoms like half the population. Nothing said "we've matched it to your top size". That's a return waiting to happen, and it'd have been "my fault" for not noticing. Rattled me slightly mid-flow.
**Next:** continued

### Step 8 — Added the full fit
**Did:** Tapped "ADD THE FULL FIT — £85".
**Got:** ...nothing visible happened. No drawer, no tick, no "added". Stayed on the product page. Eventually spotted the bag counter in the header now said [1]. (04-11-after-add.png)
**Expected:** A cart drawer sliding in, or at least a confirmation flash.
**Felt:** Did that work? And why does the bag say ONE — I just bought two things. For a good few seconds I genuinely considered pressing the button again, which would've doubled the order. Tapped the bag instead to check. That's the shakiest moment of the whole visit.
**Next:** hesitated

### Step 9 — Checked the bag
**Did:** Opened the cart page. (04-12-cart-top.png)
**Got:** One line: "CELLBLOCK SET — CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK CREWNECK - M / CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK SHORTS - L — £85.00". Both garments, both my sizes, in writing. Estimated total £85.00 GBP. Up top a bar: "Free Tracked 24 — unlocked". Pay in 3 of £28.33 offered. The thumbnail only shows the crewneck.
**Expected:** Two separate lines, £50 and £45, with a minus-£10 somewhere.
**Felt:** So the "1" was the SET counted as one item — fine once you see it, unnerving before you do. The line itself is exactly what I meant to buy, sizes and all, so all doubt gone. Two small gripes: the £10 saving has vanished — no crossed-out £95 here, so the cart doesn't remind me I got a deal — and the picture only shows the jumper, which had me re-reading the text to confirm the shorts were really in there.
**Next:** continued

### Step 10 — Pressed checkout
**Did:** Tapped the big "Check out" button under the total. (04-15-cart-checkout-btn.png)
**Got:** Real checkout, order total £85.00, express options (Shop Pay / PayPal / Google Pay), email and delivery form, United Kingdom pre-set. (04-16-checkout-landed.png)
**Expected:** Exactly this.
**Felt:** No surprises, right number, normal Shopify checkout — after all the evidence-terminal styling it's almost reassuring that the money part looks boring and standard. I'd have paid here. That's £85 they got out of a £50 intention, mostly because one tap showed me the struck-out £95.
**Next:** continued (stopped at payment — audit ends here)

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Bought (reached checkout with the CELLBLOCK SET, crewneck M + shorts L, £85) — came for a £50 jumper, left committing to £85. The struck-through £95 → £85 after ticking, plus the separate shorts size row, is what converted the upsell.
**Total time:** ~7 minutes, a minute of which was self-inflicted maths-checking and another spent wondering whether the add had worked.
**Worst moment:** The silent size-mirroring — "if I hadn't looked I'd have got medium shorts" — closely followed by tapping ADD THE FULL FIT and getting no confirmation at all while the bag badge read [1] for a two-garment purchase ("did that work? do I press it again?").
**Best moment:** The tick itself: one tap turned a vague "Save £10" into "£95 £85 for the set" with its own shorts size row and a button that says the final price. The whole offer became legible in a second — genuinely the best-explained bundle I've met, once it's open.
**Would they come back?** Yes — the set felt like good value, delivery promises are concrete, and checkout was frictionless. He'd also tell a mate "check the shorts size it picks for you".
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Nothing stopped the sale this time — but the two near-misses are one fix each: don't silently default the partner garment to the top's size (or at least label it "matched to your size — change?"), and give ANY visible confirmation when the set lands in the bag. Either of those failing differently loses the order or causes a return. Also worth carrying the "£95 £85" strike-through into the cart line — the deal disappears from view exactly when the buyer is deciding whether to press checkout. And put the bundle price on the collapsed line itself ("Save £10 — £85 for both") so trusting it doesn't require the shopper's own arithmetic.
