# 06 — Ree, 27, been watching the V2 Baggies since the drop, finally came to get them in an M
**Device:** iPhone-size mobile (390×844), home wifi, Tuesday ~21:30 · **Goal:** buy the V2 Baggies in M tonight, done deal · **Mood:** decided, card in hand — this isn't a browse, it's a pickup

### Step 1 — Opened the site from the search bar
**Did:** Went straight to the CROOKSLDN homepage.
**Got:** Black police-terminal screen, "> 14 PRODUCTS AVAILABLE TO PURCHASE", big CROOKSLDN / OWN THE STREETS™, and a COOKIE CONSENT panel parked over the bottom half. Hit Decline without reading it. (06-01-home-arrival.png, 06-02-home-clear.png)
**Expected:** The shop. Maybe a popup to swat away.
**Felt:** "14 products available to purchase" — good, they're telling me stock exists before I've even scrolled. That's the kind of thing I'm here to check.
**Next:** continued

### Step 2 — Scrolled the catalogue hunting the baggies
**Did:** Flicked down the catalogue, past the crewneck, the jeans, the tees, watching the numbered cards go by until NO. 11 — SWEATS — V2 BAGGIES, £60.00.
**Got:** The card says **AVAILABLE** in purple. So did every single other card I passed — all fourteen say AVAILABLE. No sizes on any card, no "low stock", nothing. (06-03-catalogue-baggies-card.png)
**Expected:** Some hint of what's actually left — even a "selling fast" tag. Other cards have "DROPPED 03.08" date tags, so they clearly can do tags.
**Felt:** AVAILABLE, lovely. Though if literally everything says AVAILABLE it's not really telling me anything, is it. Didn't think about that until later.
**Next:** continued

### Step 3 — Tapped the card
**Did:** Tapped the V2 BAGGIES card.
**Got:** Product page: big photo of the white baggies, £60.00, and a sticky bar at the bottom already reading "£60.00 · XS" before I'd touched a thing. Scrolled to the size row: XS and S in solid boxes — M, L and XL in dashed boxes with a line through them. "IN STOCK" written under the row, but that's for the XS it picked for me. (06-04-pdp-arrival.png, 06-05-size-row-before.png)
**Expected:** The card said AVAILABLE thirty seconds ago. I expected my size.
**Felt:** You're joking. Available meant "we have some XS left". The card upstairs told me AVAILABLE and the truth was three of five sizes gone. Why did I have to come in here to find that out?
**Next:** hesitated

### Step 4 — Tapped M anyway
**Did:** Tapped the crossed-out M, because I didn't come this far not to.
**Got:** M went purple, red text "SIZE M IS SOLD OUT", the buy button turned into a dead SOLD OUT, and a red-outlined box appeared: "TELL ME WHEN THIS SIZE IS BACK", an email field, and a purple NOTIFY ME button. The sticky bar now says "£60.00 · M" with SOLD OUT next to a still-lit CHECKOUT NOW. (06-06-M-selected.png, 06-07-notify-form.png)
**Expected:** Dead button, maybe. The instant honest "it's sold out, leave your email" was better than expected.
**Felt:** At least it's straight with me. And "when this size is BACK" — that reads like a promise it's coming back. Nothing about when, though. Next week? Next year? It's one sentence and a box; I'm signing up to something with no terms at all.
**Next:** continued

### Step 5 — Left my email
**Did:** Typed audit-restock@example.com and hit NOTIFY ME. (06-08b-email-typed-clean.png)
**Got:** A beat of nothing — then the whole page dimmed under a white overlay: "Drag the shape into its outline", a black tetris-piece thing, a trippy purple-green picture with four dashed outlines on it, a Skip button, and at one point a "Please try again ⚠️" before I'd even touched anything. (06-10-captcha-appeared.png, 06-11-captcha-loaded.png)
**Expected:** A tick. "Sorted, we'll email you." It's an email address for THEIR mailing list, not my online banking.
**Felt:** A puzzle. At half nine at night, to give a clothes shop my email so they can tell me when trousers exist. And the first thing it says is "try again" like I've already failed. I'm not dragging shapes for this.
**Next:** hesitated

### Step 6 — Gave up on the puzzle
**Did:** Stared at it, thumb hovering, then tapped the dark bit outside the box to make it go away.
**Got:** Puzzle gone. My email still sitting in the field, NOTIFY ME still purple like nothing happened. No tick, no "you're on the list", no error either. The little captcha logo still floating in the corner. (06-12-after-dismiss.png)
**Expected:** Some sign of whether that counted or not.
**Felt:** So am I signed up? Genuinely no idea. Pretty sure not. The page looks exactly like it did before I typed anything. If a restock email never comes I won't know if that's because there's no restock or because this box ate my email.
**Next:** continued

### Step 7 — Hedged like it's a sneaker drop
**Did:** Took an actual screenshot of the page on my phone (proof I tried, and so I remember the name), then scrolled to the bottom looking for their socials.
**Got:** Footer has INSTAGRAM and TIKTOK links (@crooksldn) and an email address — crooksldn@gmail.com. Followed the Instagram; that's the only restock alert I actually trust now. (06-14-pdp-footer.png)
**Expected:** Exactly this — small brand, the real news is always on the gram.
**Felt:** The notify box was meant to save me doing this, and here I am doing it anyway.
**Next:** continued

### Step 8 — Looked for anything saying whether it ever comes back
**Did:** Opened ITEM DESCRIPTION and SPECIFICATION on the product page, reading for anything about restocks or runs.
**Got:** Fabric weight, delivery times, a height chart, "Made in Portugal". Nothing about restocking. Only clues: a "MORE FROM THIS DROP" row below, and those "DROPPED 03.08" dates on the catalogue — drop-culture language, but nobody actually says what happens when a drop sells through. (06-13-item-description.png)
**Expected:** One line. "Runs are limited" or "restocks monthly" — anything.
**Felt:** They talk like a drops brand but won't say the quiet part: does sold out mean gone forever? Because if it does, that notify box is writing cheques it'll never cash.
**Next:** continued

### Step 9 — Tried the FAQ
**Did:** Tapped QUESTIONS in the footer, scanned every section — DELIVERY, SIZING, RETURNS AND REFUNDS, ORDERS AND PAYMENT — and opened the likely ones.
**Got:** Fifteen questions about postage, refunds and payment. Not one about restocks, sold-out sizes, or how drops work. The page literally opens with "The things people ask us most." (06-15-faq-top.png, 06-16-faq-expanded.png)
**Expected:** "WILL YOU RESTOCK?" is surely the single most asked question at a brand where three of five sizes are gone.
**Felt:** How is "when's my refund" in here but not "will my size come back"? I did the full lap and the site never answers it. Nowhere.
**Next:** continued

### Step 10 — One last thing on the way out
**Did:** Back on the homepage, scrolled past the packaging stuff and found REGISTER AS INFORMANT — "Drops go to the register before they go public. One message per drop, nothing else." A text-message list. (06-17-informant-block.png)
**Got:** A phone-number signup for NEW drops. Not restocks — but if the baggies ever return it'd probably be announced as a drop, so this is weirdly the closest thing the site has to an answer.
**Expected:** —
**Felt:** Funny that the SMS list tells me more about how this brand actually works — everything is drops — than anything on the product page did. Didn't sign up. One captcha ambush a night is enough; didn't fancy finding out what guards this one.
**Next:** gave up

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Didn't — M sold out (L and XL too). Tried to join the restock list, got ambushed by a drag-the-shape puzzle, bailed, and left with zero confirmation of whether the signup counted. Followed the Instagram as the real fallback.
**Total time:** ~8 minutes — and about half of that was hunting for an answer ("will it come back?") the site never gives.
**Worst moment:** Tapping outside the captcha and seeing the form sit there untouched, email still in the box: "so did that go through or not?" — the shop almost certainly lost the email of someone who was ready to pay £60 tonight.
**Best moment:** Tapping the crossed-out M and getting an instant, honest "SIZE M IS SOLD OUT" with the notify box right there — the sold-out state itself is the best-designed thing on the page.
**Would they come back?** Via Instagram, yes — they followed @crooksldn and genuinely want the trousers. Via the site's notify email, never — they don't believe it exists. And the catalogue's blanket AVAILABLE labels lost a bit of trust: next time they'll assume those mean nothing.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Let the notify email land and say so — no puzzle, and a visible "You're on the list for M. If this run comes back, you'll be first to know" (with one honest line about whether runs restock, here or in the FAQ). The sale was lost to stock; the *relationship* was lost to an unconfirmed form.
