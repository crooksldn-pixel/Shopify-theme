# 01 — Jordan, 24, saw the baggies on an Instagram story, never heard of the brand
**Device:** iPhone-size mobile (390×844), slow 4G on the bus · **Goal:** what is this, what's it cost, do they have L, is this shop legit, when would it arrive · **Mood:** idle, half-interested, ready to close the tab in 90 seconds

### Step 1 — Tapped through from the Instagram story
**Did:** Landed straight on the V2 BAGGIES product page from the story link.
**Got:** Page took a good few seconds to come in on bus signal. Then: near-black screen, monospace type like a police computer, big photo of white sweatpants, "PRODUCT 11 / 14 — SWEATS", a top bar saying FREE UK SHIPPING OVER £20 — ORDER BY 18:00 FOR SAME-DAY DISPATCH. And a huge COOKIE CONSENT box sitting over the whole bottom half of the screen. (01-01-arrival.png)
**Expected:** A normal shop page with the trousers and a price.
**Felt:** The look is actually hard — like an evidence terminal, kind of cool, doesn't feel like a scam template. But I can't see a price yet because the cookie thing is covering half my screen.
**Next:** continued

### Step 2 — Got rid of the cookie box
**Did:** Tapped Decline without reading it.
**Got:** Whole page in one go: V2 BAGGIES, £60.00, size row XS–XL, ADD TO BAG, and a sticky bar at the bottom with the price and a CHECKOUT NOW button. Photo, price, sizes — all on one screen. (01-02-after-cookie.png)
**Expected:** To have to scroll around hunting for the price.
**Felt:** Fair play, that's everything I wanted to know in about three seconds. £60 for joggers is steep for a brand I've never heard of, but they look good.
**Next:** continued

### Step 3 — Looked for my size
**Did:** Scanned the size row for L.
**Got:** M, L and XL are all crossed out with dashed boxes. Only XS and S are live. Also clocked the sticky bar already said "£60.00 · XS" before I'd touched anything, like it had picked a size for me.
**Expected:** L to be there. It's the normal size.
**Felt:** Course it's gone. All the real sizes are gone and they're left with the tiny ones. Either this brand is blowing up or they made about ten pairs. The bar saying "XS" on its own was a bit weird — I never picked that.
**Next:** hesitated

### Step 4 — Tapped L anyway
**Did:** Tapped the crossed-out L, just to see.
**Got:** L highlighted purple, red text "SIZE L IS SOLD OUT", the buy button flipped to SOLD OUT, and a box appeared: "TELL ME WHEN THIS SIZE IS BACK" with an email field and NOTIFY ME. (01-05b-L-soldout-form.png)
**Expected:** Nothing, honestly — crossed-out buttons usually just sit there.
**Felt:** OK that's clean. It tells me straight it's gone and gives me a way to hear about a restock instead of pretending. Annoyed about the stock, not about the page.
**Next:** continued

### Step 5 — Tried CHECKOUT NOW anyway
**Did:** The CHECKOUT NOW button in the bottom bar still looked lit up next to SOLD OUT, so I tapped it — half hoping it'd let me buy anyway.
**Got:** Absolutely nothing. No message, no shake, no "pick another size". Bag stayed [0]. (01-06-checkout-now-soldout.png)
**Expected:** Either take me somewhere or tell me why not.
**Felt:** Why is that button still glowing if it's dead? For a second I thought my phone hadn't registered the tap and I did it twice.
**Next:** continued

### Step 6 — Checked delivery, since I was here
**Did:** Opened "CHAIN OF CUSTODY — SHIPPING & RETURNS" further down.
**Got:** Same-day dispatch before 18:00 Mon–Sat, Royal Mail Tracked, free UK shipping over £20, UK 1–2 working days, 14 days to return. Returns by emailing crooksldn@gmail.com. (01-07-shipping-accordion.png)
**Expected:** Vague "3–5 business days" waffle.
**Felt:** That's proper — next-day-ish and free at £60. The gmail address for returns made me pause though. Big design, gmail returns inbox. Small operation then. Not a dealbreaker, but noted.
**Next:** continued

### Step 7 — Left my email for the restock
**Did:** Typed my email into the "tell me when this size is back" box and hit NOTIFY ME. (01-09-notify-typed.png)
**Got:** A massive bright-white puzzle popped over the dark page: "Find and click ALL animal icons the specified number of times" — a grid of cartoon octopuses and owls. (01-10-notify-submitted.png)
**Expected:** A tick and "we'll email you". It's one email address, not a bank login.
**Felt:** You're joking. I'm giving YOU my email so you can sell ME trousers, and I've got to do animal homework at half nine on the bus? Stared at it, hit nothing, closed it. No idea if I'm signed up or not — pretty sure not.
**Next:** gave up

### Step 8 — Last look and out
**Did:** Scrolled back up, looked at the baggies once more, closed the tab. (01-08-final-look.png)
**Got:** Same nice photo, same dead L.
**Expected:** —
**Felt:** Good trousers, real shop, wrong size, and they made the one thing I could actually do (leave an email) annoying. If it comes round on my feed again in my size I'd probably go for it.
**Next:** gave up

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Didn't — size L sold out (M and XL too; only XS/S left). Tried to join the restock list instead and bounced off a captcha puzzle, so the shop likely didn't even capture the email of a warm lead.
**Total time:** ~3 minutes (the core questions — what/price/size/legit/delivery — were answered inside the first 40 seconds, which is genuinely good).
**Worst moment:** The animal-clicking captcha after submitting the restock email — "you want me to do a puzzle to give you my email?" Close second: CHECKOUT NOW still looking active with a sold-out size selected and doing silently nothing when tapped.
**Best moment:** After dismissing the cookie box, name, price, sizes, photo and both buy buttons all fit on one screen — and the sold-out state is honest: red "SIZE L IS SOLD OUT" plus an instant notify box, no pretending.
**Would they come back?** Maybe — the brand look landed and delivery terms are strong, but only if the algorithm resurfaces it; the restock email will never come because the captcha killed the signup.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Stock in L, obviously — but the recoverable one: let the restock email go through without a visible captcha puzzle (and grey out CHECKOUT NOW when the selected size is sold out). That email was the whole conversion this session could have produced.
