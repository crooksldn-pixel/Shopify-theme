# 06 — Tobi, came for the V2 BAGGIES in M and nothing else will do

**Device:** mobile 390×844, ordinary 4G · **Goal:** buy the £60 V2 BAGGIES sweatpants in a medium · **Mood:** already decided, wallet out, expecting a two-minute job

---

### Step 1 — Opened the site cold, one thumb, standing up
**Did:** Loaded the homepage. Waited for it to draw itself.
**Got:** A thin line across the top: `FREE UK SHIPPING OVER £20 — ORDER BY 18:00 FOR SAME-DAY DISPATCH`. Handcuffs logo, `CROOKSLDN`, `OWN THE STREETS™`, a purple `CATALOGUE` button, and `> 12 PRODUCTS AVAILABLE TO PURCHASE`. Then a `COOKIE CONSENT` panel slid up and took the bottom 40% of the screen — "We and our partners, including Shopify, use cookies and other technologies to personalize your experience, show you ads, and perform analytics…" with `Accept`, `Decline` and `Manage preferences`. Nothing else popped up — no email overlay, no discount thing. (`audit/screens/06-01-home-cold.png`)
**Expected:** A shop I could get into.
**Felt:** The shipping line at the top is the first thing I read and it's the right first thing — free over £20, out today if I order before six. That's a good opening sentence. The cookie box is Shopify's own paragraph in this shop's typeface, which is a bit of a seam, but `Decline` is a real button next to `Accept` and not buried, so I didn't resent it.
**Next:** continued

### Step 2 — Declined cookies and tapped CATALOGUE
**Did:** `Decline`, then the purple `CATALOGUE` button.
**Got:** The register: `12 ITEMS`, then numbered rows — `NO. 01 SWEATS CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK CREWNECK £50.00 AVAILABLE`, and so on down. (`audit/screens/06-03-home-clear.png`, `audit/screens/06-04-catalogue.png`)
**Expected:** A grid of pictures.
**Felt:** It's a list of evidence, not a shop, and I like it. Every row has a price and a status word on it before I've tapped anything.
**Next:** continued

### Step 3 — Found the baggies and read the status word next to them
**Did:** Scrolled to number nine.
**Got:** `NO. 09 / SWEATS / V2 BAGGIES / £60.00` — and where every other row says `AVAILABLE`, this one says **`2 OF 5 SIZES LEFT`**. (`audit/screens/06-04-catalogue.png`)
**Expected:** `AVAILABLE`, like the rest.
**Felt:** Stomach drop, and credit where it's due — the shop told me before I tapped. Two of five left is not a fake scarcity counter, it's just a fact, and it's sitting in the same slot where the other twelve products say `AVAILABLE`. I knew there was a real chance my size was gone before I'd committed to the page. Most shops make you find that out three taps later.
**Next:** continued, braced

### Step 4 — Opened the product
**Did:** Tapped the row.
**Got:** `← CATALOGUE`, `PRODUCT 09 / 12`, `SWEATS`, a big clean photo, `PHOTO 1 OF 2`, then `V2 BAGGIES` `£60.00`, `SIZE`, and a row of five boxes: `XS S M L XL`. Under it: `Select Size`, a `SIZE GUIDE` button, `Order before 18:00 and it ships today (Mon–Sat)`, `> Ordered now — leaves tomorrow`, and a full-width `SELECT A SIZE` button. (`audit/screens/06-05-pdp-top.png`)
**Expected:** This.
**Felt:** Good page. No badges, no "17 people are viewing this", nothing shouting. The pants look like the pants.
**Next:** continued

### Step 5 — Looked at the size row before touching it, because of that "2 of 5"
**Did:** Stared at the five boxes.
**Got:** `XS` and `S` sit in solid boxes in bright text. `M`, `L` and `XL` are in **dashed** boxes, in dimmer grey, with a **line through the letter**. (`audit/screens/06-06b-size-row-closeup.png`, `audit/screens/06-06-size-row-before-tap.png`)
**Expected:** Sold-out sizes to be greyed or missing.
**Felt:** Yes — I could tell before I tapped. Struck through, dashed, dimmer: three signals, all of them quiet. Quiet is right for this shop, and I did read it. But I'll be honest that in bright sun the strike through a single capital `M` is a thin grey line on near-black, and the thing I *actually* clocked first was that XS and S looked brighter than the rest. It landed. It landed softly.
**Next:** hesitated

### Step 6 — Tapped M anyway, because you do
**Did:** Tapped the `M` box.
**Got:** It selected — purple fill, still struck through — and the page rearranged: in red, **`SIZE M IS SOLD OUT`**. The buy button went grey and now says **`SOLD OUT`**. A red-outlined panel appeared under it: **`TELL ME WHEN THIS SIZE IS BACK`**, a field placeheld `email address`, and a purple **`NOTIFY ME`**. The bar at the bottom updated to `V2 BAGGIES  £60.00 · M   SOLD OUT   CHECKOUT NOW`. (`audit/screens/06-07-M-selected.png`)
**Expected:** Either nothing at all, or a "sorry, sold out".
**Felt:** This is the best-behaved thing on the site. The size stayed tappable, so I got a straight answer instead of a dead box that ignores my thumb, and the answer is one plain sentence with my actual size in it. And — I checked twice because I expected the opposite — **the shipping promise switched itself off**. `Order before 18:00 and it ships today` and `Ordered now — leaves tomorrow` both vanished the moment M was selected. The shop stopped promising me a delivery date for a thing it can't send. That's honest, and I noticed.
**Next:** continued

### Step 7 — Read the notify panel properly before giving them my email
**Did:** Read every word in the red box. Then read it again looking for the bit I'd missed.
**Got:** Three things, in full: `TELL ME WHEN THIS SIZE IS BACK` / `email address` / `NOTIFY ME`. That is the entire text. (`audit/screens/06-08-notify-panel.png`)
**Expected:** A line telling me what I'm signing up for. "We'll email you once, when M is back." Something.
**Felt:** So — what have I just been offered, exactly? "This size" doesn't name the size anywhere in the box, so I'm trusting that it knows I mean M and not the L I looked at ten seconds ago. Nobody says whether I get one email or a mailing list. Nobody says when, or whether "back" is next week or never. There's no "we won't spam you", no privacy line, nothing about what happens to my address. It's a black box with the word BACK in it. I gave them the address anyway, because I want the trousers — but I gave it the way you give a stranger your number, not the way you sign up for something.
**Next:** continued

**Next:** hesitated

### Step 9 — Then a blank white sheet covered the phone
**Did:** Kept watching.
**Got:** The whole product page went grey and a **completely blank white rectangle** — roughly 320×490, no text, no border, no close button, no spinner — dropped over the middle of the screen and stayed there for about ten seconds. Then it disappeared on its own. Underneath, nothing had changed: `SIZE M IS SOLD OUT`, the same red panel, my email still sitting in the field. No thank-you. No error. (`audit/screens/06-11-after-submit-1-11s.png`)
**Expected:** Anything with words on it.
**Felt:** This is the moment the shop lost me, and I want to be exact about why. It isn't that a robot check appeared — I've ticked a hundred of those. It's that it was **blank**. A white box with nothing in it, on top of a black page, for ten seconds, on a phone. I genuinely did not know whether it was loading, whether it had crashed, whether it wanted me to tap it, or whether it *was* the confirmation and the confirmation just hadn't rendered. Then it vanished and left me on the same screen I started on. My honest read, out loud, was *"did that do anything?"*

If you asked me to bet, I'd bet nothing was recorded. A shop that wanted my email would tell me it had got it. So my conclusion as a normal person is either the site is broken, or they don't actually want to hear from me — and honestly, from the outside those two look the same.

## Outcome

**Bought / didn't:** Didn't — I couldn't. M is sold out and so is everything above it. But that isn't the failure. The failure is that I was willing to leave my email and wait, I pressed the button four times to do it, and the shop never once acknowledged that I'd pressed anything. I left not knowing whether I'm on a list or not.

**Total time:** About eight minutes, and six of them were spent on a form.

**Worst moment:** Step 9 — the blank white rectangle. A wordless white box over a greyed page for ten seconds, then gone, with my email still sitting in the field and nothing said. *"Did that do anything?"* I still don't know. And the fact that presses three and four produced **no** white box and **no** message at all is somehow worse than the box: the same button did two different nothings. If you want the single sentence a shopper would say about this store, it's *"they've got a button for telling you when it's back and pressing it does nothing."*

**Best moment:** Genuinely two. First, `2 OF 5 SIZES LEFT` on the catalogue row, sitting where every other product says `AVAILABLE` — I was warned before I tapped in, without a fake countdown or a scarcity gimmick, and that is exactly the right amount of information at exactly the right moment. Second, and I did not expect it: **the shipping promise turns itself off.** The instant I picked a sold-out size, `Order before 18:00 and it ships today` and `Ordered now — leaves tomorrow` disappeared. A lot of shops would have left that sitting there under a `SOLD OUT` button. This one didn't, and that's the difference between a shop that's out of stock and a shop that's lying to you.

**Would they come back?** For the clothes, yes — the trousers are good, the price is on the page, the size grid answers instantly and the measurements are one tap away. But I'd come back **on my own**, by remembering to, because I do not believe an email is coming. And I'd come back with less faith than I arrived with, because the one thing I tried to do here failed silently four times.

**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Make `NOTIFY ME` say something. Anything, in the shop's own voice, in the same red box, the moment it's pressed — `LOGGED — WE WILL EMAIL buyer@… WHEN SIZE M RETURNS. ONE EMAIL, NOTHING ELSE.` That single line does three jobs at once: it tells me it worked, it tells me *which size* I signed up for, and it tells me what I've agreed to receive. Right now the panel says `TELL ME WHEN THIS SIZE IS BACK` and then never tells me anything again — not what I signed up for, not when I'd hear, not even that it heard me. Fix the silence and this becomes the moment I forgive the shop for being out of stock; leave it and £60 walks out of the door with no way back in.
