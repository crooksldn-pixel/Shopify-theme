# 09 — Priya, buying her younger brother a birthday present and has no idea what size he wears

**Device:** desktop 1440×900, normal broadband · **Goal:** spend about £60 on something he'll actually like, without having to ask him his size and ruin the surprise · **Mood:** organised, slightly rushed, doing this on a work laptop between meetings. Would much rather buy a gift card and be done.

---

### Step 1 — Landed on the homepage from a link he'd posted
**Did:** Opened the site cold. Waited while it drew itself in — a couple of seconds where the top half was just black.
**Got:** A black terminal-looking page. `CROOKSLDN`, `OWN THE STREETS™`, a purple `CATALOGUE` button, and a line above it reading `> 12 PRODUCTS AVAILABLE TO PURCHASE`. A cookie bar took the bottom fifth of the screen: `COOKIE CONSENT` / "We and our partners, including Shopify, use cookies and other technologies to personalize your experience, show you ads, and perform analytics…" with `Manage preferences` / `Accept` / `Decline`. It sat right on top of the `FLAT` / `ON MODEL` / `OUTLINE` buttons. (`09-01-home-arrival.png`)
**Expected:** A shop. Maybe a "gifts" link in the top bar, the way most places have one in December.
**Felt:** It looks cool, I'll give it that — it doesn't look like every other clothing site. But there's a lot of black and not much telling me what to do. I hit Accept without reading it, like everyone does.
**Next:** continued

### Step 2 — Read the top bar for anything that would solve my problem
**Did:** Scanned the header: `CATALOGUE`, `SEARCH`, `BAG [0]`, `LIGHT MODE`, `MENU`.
**Got:** Five things. None of them a gift anything. (`09-02-home-fold.png`)
**Expected:** Honestly, on a small brand, I half expected a "GIFT CARD" link sitting right there. It's the easiest sale they'll ever make.
**Felt:** Fine. It's a small label, not John Lewis. I'll go looking.
**Next:** continued

### Step 3 — Scrolled the catalogue to see what £60 buys
**Did:** Scrolled down through the products.
**Got:** Twelve items, numbered `NO. 01` to `NO. 12`, £6 to £60. `BLUE WASH OG JEANS £60.00`, `GREY WASH OG JEANS £60.00`, `V2 BAGGIES £60.00`, a `CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK CREWNECK £50.00`, tees at £25, socks at £6, a `LARGE DUFFLE BAG £18.00`. Every single one either has sizes or is socks. (`09-03-home-full.png`)
**Expected:** Something one-size and giftable — a cap, a keyring, a card.
**Felt:** This is exactly my problem in one screen. Everything good is £50–£60 and everything at £50–£60 needs a size I don't have. The duffle bag at £18 is the only sizeless thing worth having and it's a third of what I want to spend.
**Next:** continued

### Step 4 — The packaging block stopped me, in a good way
**Did:** Kept scrolling and hit a section headed `EVERY ORDER SHIPS LIKE THIS`.
**Got:** "Sealed, tagged and numbered before it leaves us. Nothing here is an extra you pay for." Then a numbered list beside a photo of the actual packaging: `01 EVIDENCE TAG — Numbered, seized by CROOKS LDN, QR to verify.` `02 SECURITY SEAL — Pull-tight, stamped with the cuffs.` `03 CUFF KEYRING * — Miniature cuffs, on the ring.` (`09-04-packaging.png`)
**Expected:** Nothing — I'd stopped expecting anything gift-shaped.
**Felt:** This is the best thing on the site for someone like me. It answers the exact worry I had — will this turn up looking like a bag of stock. Numbered tag, sealed, a little keyring. That's a present. I'd almost forgive them not having wrapping.
**Next:** continued

### Step 5 — Then read the small print under it and deflated slightly
**Did:** Read the footnote at the bottom of that block.
**Got:** `* CONTRABAND 03 SHIPS WITH SWEAT BOTTOMS ONLY.`
**Expected:** That the three things listed were what turns up.
**Felt:** So the keyring — the only bit of that which reads as a gift rather than as security theatre — only comes with sweat bottoms. I'm buying jeans. So I get a tag and a cable tie. They led with the nicest thing and then took it away in an asterisk. I had to read it twice to work out what "CONTRABAND 03" even meant.
**Next:** continued

### Step 6 — Went to the footer, which is where gift cards usually live
**Did:** Scrolled to the very bottom to read the footer links.
**Got:** Halfway there the whole page went dark and a box appeared: `CRACK THE CUFFS.` — "10% off your first order if you do. Three tumblers. Click each one at the right moment." with `RUN IT` and `NOT NOW`, and underneath "One code per player. Attempts unlimited. Code expires in 20 minutes." It landed square on top of the footer I was reading. There were two close crosses on screen at once — one on the box, one floating in the top-right corner. (`09-06-footer.png`)
**Expected:** To read a footer.
**Felt:** A game. For 10% off. I'm at work, I'm buying a birthday present, and it wants me to play a minigame with a twenty-minute timer. I clicked the X. If I'd been on my phone I might have left there. And note what the one interruption on the whole site was for — a discount code — when the thing I actually wanted was a gift card, and there isn't one.
**Next:** hesitated, then continued

### Step 7 — Actually read the footer
**Did:** Read all four footer columns.
**Got:** `SHOP` (NEW, TEES, DENIM, SWEATS, ACCESSORIES) · `INFORMATION` (QUESTIONS, TERMS, SHIPPING, REFUNDS, TRACK ORDER) · `CONTACT` (INSTAGRAM, TIKTOK, EMAIL) · `GAME` (Play CROOKSLDN: The Getaway). Then `EVIDENCE TERMINAL V0.2 // CROOKSLDN // OWN THE STREETS™ // © 2026`. (`09-06-footer.png`)
**Expected:** A `GIFT CARDS` link in the INFORMATION or SHOP column. That is where every shop I have ever bought a gift card from keeps it.
**Felt:** Fourteen links and one of them is a video game. Not one is a gift card. There's a whole footer column dedicated to a game and nothing at all for the person trying to give them money for someone else.
**Next:** continued

### Step 8 — Opened MENU, in case it was hiding in there
**Did:** Clicked `MENU` in the header.
**Got:** A panel slid in from the right: `SHOP` → ALL / NEW / TEES / DENIM / SWEATS / TRACKSUITS / ACCESSORIES, then TRACKING, QUESTIONS, TERMS, CONTACT — and below all of that, taking up the whole bottom of the panel, a little animated pixel game of a thief moving round a prison collecting coins. (`09-45-menu-drawer.png`, `09-46-menu-drawer-full.png`)
**Expected:** A gift card entry under SHOP.
**Felt:** Same answer, twelve links, none of them gifts. The game gets more physical space in this menu than anything to do with buying, which is a choice. It did make me smile, briefly, before I remembered what I was doing.
**Next:** continued

### Step 9 — Gave up browsing and used the search box
**Did:** Clicked `SEARCH` in the header, landed on the search page, typed `gift card`.
**Got:** The box was already focused, placeholder `Item, category or question`, helper text `SEARCH BY ITEM, CATEGORY OR COLOUR`. Result: `SEARCH: GIFT CARD` / `0 RESULTS` / `NO ITEMS IN THE REGISTER MATCH THAT QUERY.` (`09-20-search-landing.png`, `09-08-search-gift-card.png`)
**Expected:** A gift card. Or at minimum "we don't do gift cards yet, here's what we suggest".
**Felt:** That is the whole reason I opened the search box, answered in four words. And "NO ITEMS IN THE REGISTER MATCH THAT QUERY" is a very cold way to be told no — no suggestion, no "try the catalogue", nothing to click. Dead end, in character, but still a dead end.
**Next:** hesitated

### Step 10 — Tried four more words for the same thing, because surely
**Did:** Searched `gift`, then `gift wrap`, then `voucher`, then `present`.
**Got:** `0 RESULTS` and `NO ITEMS IN THE REGISTER MATCH THAT QUERY.` for every one of them. (`09-08-search-gift.png`, `09-08-search-gift-wrap.png`, `09-08-search-voucher.png`, `09-08-search-present.png`)
**Expected:** By the third one, nothing.
**Felt:** Right. So there is no gift card, no gift wrap, and the word "gift" does not appear anywhere on this website. That is a decision someone has made, whether they realise it or not.
**Next:** continued — now trying to solve the size problem instead

### Step 11 — Searched `size guide`, and got told two opposite things at once
**Did:** Typed `size guide` into the same box.
**Got:** Above the results, a block headed `DIRECT LINKS` offering `SIZE GUIDE` (tagged `SIZING`). Below it, on the same screen: `SEARCH: SIZE GUIDE` / `0 RESULTS` / `NO ITEMS IN THE REGISTER MATCH THAT QUERY.` (`09-08-search-size-guide.png`, `09-21-typeahead-size-guide.png`)
**Expected:** A size guide.
**Felt:** One half of the screen is handing me a size guide and the other half is telling me there's nothing here. I know which one I believed for about two seconds — the big heading that says zero. It was only because the small purple link was above it that I clicked at all.
**Next:** continued

### Step 12 — Followed the SIZE GUIDE link
**Did:** Clicked `SIZE GUIDE` under DIRECT LINKS.
**Got:** Dropped at the top of `/pages/faq` — `ENQUIRIES` / `COMMONLY ASKED QUESTIONS` — with every question collapsed. No size chart on screen. I had to find `SIZING` in a list of fifteen closed questions and open `HOW DO I KNOW WHAT SIZE TO BUY?` myself. (`09-22-size-guide-destination.png`, `09-23-faq-top.png`)
**Expected:** A size chart. The link said SIZE GUIDE.
**Felt:** That's not a size guide, that's a FAQ page. Nothing was even scrolled to the right bit — I landed at the top and had to hunt. Two clicks that should have been none.
**Next:** continued

### Step 13 — Read the sizing answer
**Did:** Opened `HOW DO I KNOW WHAT SIZE TO BUY?`
**Got:** "Where a piece has been measured, its product page carries a measurements table — tap SIZE GUIDE next to the size buttons. Everything is measured with the garment laid flat, and you can switch the table between centimetres and inches." And then, genuinely nicely: "If the piece you want is not listed yet, message us and we will measure it for you." (`09-38-faq-how-do-i-know-what-size-.png`)
**Expected:** A chart mapping S/M/L to a body.
**Felt:** So there's no size guide as such — there's a measurements table per product. Which is fine if I have the person, or one of their jumpers, in front of me. I have neither. He's forty minutes away. The offer to measure something for me is lovely and completely useless when the problem is that I don't know *his* measurements, not the garment's.
**Next:** continued

### Step 14 — Checked the exchange policy before going any further
**Did:** Opened `DO YOU DO EXCHANGES?` in the same FAQ, then read `03 RETURNS` and `04 SIZE SWAPS` on the Terms page.
**Got:** FAQ: "Yes. You pay the postage sending the original back to us. There is no fee for the swap itself, and we cover the postage sending the new size out to you. Start it in the returns centre and say which size you want — swaps depend on that size being in stock, and if it is not we refund you instead." Terms `04 SIZE SWAPS` says the same in the same words. Terms `03 RETURNS`: "You have 14 days from delivery to tell us you want to return something, and 14 days from then to post it back." (`09-38-faq-do-you-do-exchanges.png`, `09-25-terms.png`)
**Expected:** Vagueness. Small brands are usually vague here.
**Felt:** This is the best-written thing on the site. It's a straight yes, it tells me exactly who pays for what, and it warns me the swap depends on stock. It's the first moment I've thought these people are serious.

But it's written for the person wearing the thing. If I give him jeans and they're wrong, *he* has to go to a returns centre with *my* order number and email, in a fortnight that starts the day it's delivered to me — not to him. Nobody has thought about the gift case. And "14 days from delivery" is a genuinely bad clock for a present bought early.
**Next:** continued

### Step 15 — Onto the jeans, and the SIZE GUIDE button on the product page

**Felt:** That worked exactly right and I want to say so. Button, scroll, table, done. It's the smoothest thing that's happened in ten minutes.
**Next:** continued

### Step 16 — Read the table and hit the wall this whole journey was heading for
**Did:** Read the measurements for the jeans, then flipped `CM` to `IN`.
**Got:** `TRUE TO SIZE — WAIST, CHEST AND LEG MEASUREMENTS ARE TAKEN AROUND THE GARMENT. ALL MEASUREMENTS IN CENTIMETRES.` Then SIZE / WAIST / INSEAM / LEG OPENING: XS 76.2 / 73.7 / 45.7, S 81.3 / 76.2 / 48.3, M 86.4 / 77.5 / 50.8, L 91.4 / 80.0 / 53.3, XL 96.5 / 81.3 / 55.9. The `IN` button redraws it as 30 / 32 / 34 / 36 / 38 waist and the sentence changes to "All measurements in inches." (`09-43-after-size-guide.png`, `09-36-measurements-in.png`)
**Expected:** Something that lets me pick a size for someone who isn't here.
**Felt:** These are the jeans' measurements, not his. There is no "M fits 32–34 waist", no height guide, no "if he wears a medium in a normal tee, take M". To use this I need to go to his flat, find a pair of his jeans and measure the waistband. That is not a thing you can do for a surprise.

Two other things nagged. The centimetres are all things like 76.2 and 86.4, which are just inches converted — it reads like a spec sheet somebody typed in inches rather than anything measured. And the note here says measurements are taken **around** the garment, while the FAQ I'd read three minutes earlier says everything is measured **laid flat**. Those are twice as different from each other. If I had gone and measured a pair of his jeans I'd have had a fifty-fifty chance of buying something twice the right size.
**Next:** hesitated hard

**Did:** Clicked `Check out` and read the payment page. Entered nothing, submitted nothing.

I *can* type his address into Delivery, so I can send it to him. But there's no way to tell him it's from me. He'd get a sealed evidence bag from a brand he likes, with a numbered tag and no idea who sent it.

## Outcome

**Bought / didn't:** Didn't. I abandoned it on the payment page with £60 of jeans in the bag, on a guessed size, with no way to say who it was from. The thing that actually stopped me was not the missing gift card on its own — it was the three of them stacking up: I can't get a gift card, I can't work out his size from garment measurements, and I can't attach a note. Any one of those I'd have worked around. All three means I'm doing the recipient's homework and carrying the risk of getting it wrong.

**Total time:** About 11 minutes, of which a good four were spent looking for a gift card in four different places before accepting there isn't one.

**Worst moment:** Searching `gift card` and getting `0 RESULTS` / `NO ITEMS IN THE REGISTER MATCH THAT QUERY.` — out loud, "you're joking, you don't do gift cards?" It's the single easiest sale a small label can make, it's the one thing that solves the size problem completely, and it doesn't exist. Close second: reading `TAKEN AROUND THE GARMENT` on the product page after the FAQ had told me `laid flat`, and realising I couldn't trust the numbers either way.

**Best moment:** Genuinely two. The `SIZE GUIDE` button on the product page — clicked once, it scrolled me down and opened the table, no popup to fight. And `EVERY ORDER SHIPS LIKE THIS`: numbered tag, security seal, "Nothing here is an extra you pay for." That paragraph is doing more gift-buyer work than the rest of the site combined, and nobody has noticed, because it's sitting on the homepage instead of anywhere near the bag.

**Would they come back?** For myself, yes — I liked it, it's got a point of view. As a present, no. **If a friend asked me where to buy him something, I'd send them somewhere with a gift card, and I'd say so plainly: "they're good but you have to know his size, so get him something else."** That's the sentence this shop is currently earning, and I'd have paid £60 today to avoid saying it.
