# 09 — Priya, aunt buying a birthday present for a nephew whose size she doesn't know
**Device:** mobile 390×844, normal network · **Goal:** her nephew turns 16 on Sunday and every second word out of him lately is "Crooks" — she wants to arrive with the right thing, or failing that a gift card, without asking him and ruining the surprise · **Mood:** on the sofa at 21:25 with one eye on the TV; expects this to take ten minutes

### Step 1 — Opened the site, got the airlock treatment
**Did:** Opened crooksldn on her phone. A full-screen "CRACK THE CUFFS — ACCESS BREACH DETECTED" game sat on top of a cookie banner she could half-see underneath. Tapped the × on the game, then Accept on the cookies.
**Got:** Two overlays down, and finally the shop: near-black, monospace, "14 PRODUCTS AVAILABLE TO PURCHASE", like a police computer. (09-01, 09-02, 09-03)
**Expected:** A normal shop. Cookie banner, fine. A handcuff mini-game offering "a private discount" was not on her bingo card.
**Felt:** "Right, this is very much HIS world and not mine. The game thing would delight him and it's wasted on me. Two doors to get through before a single product."
**Next:** continued

### Step 2 — Scrolled the whole homepage looking for the word "gift"
**Did:** Scrolled top to bottom, reading the catalogue grid (numbered NO. 01–14 like evidence records), then a section called PROPERTY BAG, then the footer. Specifically looking for "gift card", "gifts", anything.
**Got:** No gift anything — not in the menu (SHOP / TRACKING / QUESTIONS / TERMS / CONTACT), not in the footer. But PROPERTY BAG stopped her: every order ships sealed in a numbered evidence bag with a security tag and a little handcuff keyring, "Nothing here is an extra you pay for." (09-03, 09-04)
**Expected:** A "Gift cards" link in the footer — every shop has one, surely.
**Felt:** "No gift card link anywhere. But hold on — the parcel itself comes as a sealed evidence bag with a tag and a keyring? That's better than any wrapping I'd do. If I can just get the SIZE right, the presentation is solved for free."
**Next:** continued — went shopping for the actual present

### Step 3 — Picked his present: the £50 crewneck. Then looked for any size help at all
**Did:** Tapped CATALOGUE, tapped NO. 01 CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK CREWNECK — £50, proper main-present money. Scrolled the whole product page hunting for a size guide, a chart, a "model wears", anything.
**Got:** Sizes XS–XL with XS already selected, IN STOCK, an offer to "Cop the full fit" with matching shorts — and three accordions: SPECIFICATION, ITEM DESCRIPTION, CHAIN OF CUSTODY. No size guide link. No measurements. Nothing. (09-11, 09-12)
**Expected:** A SIZE GUIDE link by the size buttons — even cheap fast-fashion sites have one.
**Felt:** "Five size buttons and not one clue what they mean. XS is pre-picked like it's daring me to get it wrong. I don't even know what size I'M looking for, that's the whole problem — and this page gives me nothing to work with."
**Next:** hesitated — went looking for help in the footer

### Step 4 — Read the FAQ: sizing, exchanges, returns
**Did:** Tapped QUESTIONS in the footer. Opened "HOW DO I KNOW WHAT SIZE TO BUY?", then "DO YOU DO EXCHANGES?", then "CAN I RETURN SOMETHING?".
**Got:** Sizing: "Where a piece has been measured, its product page carries a measurements table — tap SIZE GUIDE next to the size buttons… If the piece you want is not listed yet, message us and we will measure it for you." Exchanges: "UK size swaps are free… we cover postage on the replacement going out. You cover the postage sending the original back." Returns: 14 days from delivery to say so, 14 more to post it back. Replies to messages "within 1–2 working days". (09-08, 09-09)
**Expected:** A link to one big size chart she could read or send.
**Felt:** "So size guides exist — 'where a piece has been measured'. Which apparently doesn't include the £50 jumper I want. And 'message us and we'll measure it' is lovely, but it's Tuesday night and the party's Sunday; 1–2 working days is a no. The free swap thing though — that's genuinely decent. That might be my safety net."
**Next:** continued — plan B: gift card

### Step 5 — Searched for a gift card and watched plan B die
**Did:** Tapped SEARCH in the header, typed "gift card", hit enter. Then tried just "gift".
**Got:** "SEARCH: GIFT CARD — 0 RESULTS. NO ITEMS IN THE REGISTER MATCH THAT QUERY." Same for "gift". (09-05, 09-06, 09-07)
**Expected:** Honestly, a gift card. A streetwear brand for teenagers that doesn't sell gift cards is turning away every aunt, uncle and grandparent in the country.
**Felt:** "You're joking. That was my guaranteed £50. 'No items in the register match that query' — very on-brand way of telling me I can't give you money safely."
**Next:** hesitated — nearly went to look for the same jumper elsewhere; gave it one more go

### Step 6 — Downgraded to a tee, found the size guide lottery
**Did:** Went to the tees. Opened the CRXST★RZ T-SHIRT (£25, "DROPPED 03.08" — new, he'd approve). Scrolled for the size guide the FAQ promised. Not there — only ITEM DESCRIPTION and CHAIN OF CUSTODY. Suspicious now, she opened a different £25 tee (MONEY CLIVE TEE) to check.
**Got:** The Money Clive tee HAS the size guide: a proper measurements table, chest/length/shoulder for XS–XL, cm/in toggle, "GARMENT LAID FLAT". The tee her nephew would actually want has nothing. Same price, same rail, pot luck. (09-14, 09-24)
**Expected:** Whatever sizing help exists to be on every item — not a lottery.
**Felt:** "So it's not 'tees don't have charts', it's 'some do, some don't', and of course HIS one doesn't. And even the good table is garment measurements — chest laid flat in centimetres. I don't know his chest measurement, I know he's about my height and lives in baggy everything. This chart is written for someone measuring themselves, not someone buying for a 16-year-old in secret. I could text him the link — and ruin it."
**Next:** continued — went to check what happens if she guesses wrong

### Step 7 — Read the fine print like a contract: what if the size is wrong?
**Did:** Footer → REFUNDS, then TERMS, where "start your return here: the returns centre" linked to an actual returns portal.
**Got:** Refunds page: "14 days from delivery to return or exchange any unworn item with tags on. Size swaps are free within the UK — however return postage is covered by the customer." Returns go to an "Oairo Uk Office" in Bourne End, contact by email or Instagram DM. The returns centre is real — but it wants the ORDER NUMBER and the ORDER EMAIL to start anything. (09-10, 09-21, 09-23)
**Expected:** A swap process the recipient could use. Maybe a gift receipt.
**Felt:** "Free size swaps — good. But it all runs through MY order number and MY email. If the medium's wrong, he can't sort it himself; he has to come back to me, I dig out the confirmation, I post it, all inside 14 days of DELIVERY — not 14 days of him opening it. Buy this three weeks before a birthday and the window's shut before the wrapping's off. Lucky his birthday's Sunday."
**Next:** continued — made her decision

### Step 8 — Guessed L, and the guess felt survivable only because of the swap policy
**Did:** Back to the CRXST★RZ tee. Stared at the five buttons. He's 16, tall-ish, wears everything huge. Tapped L. Black. ADD TO BAG.
**Got:** "Added — 1 in bag · View bag." The sticky bar read £25.00 · L. (09-16, 09-17)
**Expected:** To feel more certain than she did. This is a coin toss with a return label attached.
**Felt:** "L, because teenagers size up and because if it's wrong the swap is free. I'm not choosing this size, I'm choosing the least-bad mistake. A £50 jumper on these odds? Absolutely not — that sale died in step 3."
**Next:** continued

### Step 9 — Padded the parcel with socks, because socks can't be the wrong size. Probably.
**Did:** Opened the WHITE/RED MOTIONTEC socks (£6) so the parcel isn't just one tee. Glanced for a size before adding.
**Got:** No size anywhere — not on the buttons (those are quantity: 1pc/3pc/6pc/12pc), and SPECIFICATION says only "Cotton blend, reinforced heel". Added 1pc. Bag: £31, free UK shipping threshold already passed. (09-18, 09-20)
**Expected:** A shoe-size range, like every sock she's ever bought — "UK 6–11" would do.
**Felt:** "Even the socks won't tell me a size. I'm assuming one-size-fits-teenager. At £6 I'll risk it, but it's a bit rich that literally nothing in this shop states a size in real-world terms."
**Next:** continued

### Step 10 — Cart: last hunt for a gift note or wrapping. Nothing.
**Did:** Opened the bag. Scrolled the whole cart page looking for "add a gift note", "this is a gift", a message box, anything to stop the invoice landing in the parcel or to add a card.
**Got:** Tee (Black, L) + socks, £31, a "£39.00 to free Tracked 24" progress bar, Check out button. No note field, no gift option of any kind. (09-19)
**Expected:** At minimum a cart note — most shops this size have the little "order note" box.
**Felt:** "So he gets an evidence bag — great — possibly with a price on the slip showing his present cost £31 — less great — and no way for me to write 'Happy 16th, don't tell your mum how much socks cost'. I'll have to write an actual card like it's 1995."
**Next:** continued

### Step 11 — Walked to the checkout door and stopped
**Did:** Tapped Check out. Scanned the checkout page for any last gift option before the payment bit.
**Got:** Standard checkout: £31.00, express pay buttons, email, delivery to United Kingdom. No gift message, no gift receipt, no "hide prices". That's the order she'd place. (09-22)
**Expected:** This, by now.
**Felt:** "Fine. Tee and socks it is — £31 out of the £56 I walked in ready to spend, and the difference is entirely because nobody would tell me what size a medium is."
**Next:** stopped at the payment step — order intent confirmed

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Bought — reached checkout with the £25 tee (Black, L — a guess underwritten by the free UK size-swap policy) and £6 socks. But the store lost the sale she came to make: the £50 crewneck was abandoned purely because it carries no size information of any kind, and there was no gift card to fall back on. £31 spent of the £50–£56 she intended.
**Total time:** ~24 minutes — most of it hunting for things that don't exist (gift card, size help on two of the three products she wanted, any gift option in the cart).
**Worst moment:** Typing "gift card" into search and getting "0 RESULTS — NO ITEMS IN THE REGISTER MATCH THAT QUERY." Out loud: "You literally sell to teenagers. Who do you think pays for teenagers?" Close second: the £50 crewneck offering five size buttons, XS pre-selected, and not one word of sizing help.
**Best moment:** The PROPERTY BAG section — every order arrives as a sealed, numbered evidence bag with a security seal and a cuff keyring, free. For a gift buyer that's accidental genius: the unboxing IS the wrapping, and her nephew will love it more than any paper she'd have chosen. The genuinely generous exchange answer in the FAQ ("UK size swaps are free… we cover postage on the replacement") is what rescued the sale at all.
**Would they come back?** Only under duress — i.e. next birthday, because it's HIS brand, not because it was easy. If the L fits she'll note his size and reorder; if it doesn't, she inherits a 14-day email-and-postage errand for a present she paid for, and next year it's cash in a card.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Sell a gift card. She arrived ready to spend £50+ and a gift card was her stated fallback — with one in the register she either buys it instantly or guesses the crewneck size knowing the gift card was there as the safe alternative next time. (A close second: put the measurements table on every sized product — the crewneck and the CRXST tee, the two things she actually wanted, are exactly the ones without it.)

## What EXISTS vs what is MISSING (gift-buyer checklist)
- Size guide: EXISTS on some products only (both jeans, V2 Baggies, Money Clive tee — flat garment measurements, cm/in toggle, well made) — MISSING on Charcoal Cellblock Crewneck, CRXST★RZ tee, Cellblock Set; no standalone size-guide page to read or send; no "model wears" info; no body-size (height/chest) guidance anywhere; socks state no size at all.
- Gift card: MISSING entirely — search returns 0 results, nothing in nav, footer or FAQ.
- Gift wrapping / gift note / gift receipt: MISSING — no option on product, cart (no note field at all) or checkout; ironically the standard packaging (evidence bag + seal + keyring) is the best "gift wrap" in the segment and is not marketed as such.
- Wrong-size path: EXISTS and is generous (14 days from delivery + 14 to post, free UK size swaps, outbound replacement postage covered, AfterShip returns portal) — but is buyer-locked (needs order number + order email, so the recipient can't self-serve) and the clock runs from delivery, not gifting.
