**Response A:**

The PDP passed me in about a second. £60.00, SIZE XS S M L XL, IN STOCK, ADD TO BAG — every word I need is plain English, and the fiction is decoration hung around a completely conventional spine. That's the whole trick and nobody should touch it.

- CHAIN OF CUSTODY — does not read as shipping. It reads as a brand-story accordion. I'd never tap it hunting for delivery, and Persona 1 step 7 confirms I didn't. The piece that made me suspicious: ACCESS BREACH DETECTED. A fake security alert, on a shop I've never heard of, before I've seen a product. That is scam-ad vocabulary. Persona 4 step 1 — guard up before seeing a garment. Fire it on exit or on second visit, never on a PDP.
