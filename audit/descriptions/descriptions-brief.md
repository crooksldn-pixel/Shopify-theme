# Council brief — rewriting the 13 live CROOKSLDN product descriptions

## The decision
Rewrite the ITEM DESCRIPTION copy for all 13 active products on CROOKSLDN
(London streetwear, police-evidence-terminal storefront), grounded in what 40
audited shopper journeys wanted. The council debates EVERY draft below and the
chairman issues final copy per product. The copy ships to the live store.

## Where this copy renders
In the PDP's "ITEM DESCRIPTION" accordion — one of four accordions. The
SPECIFICATION accordion beside it ALREADY displays the metafields verbatim
(fabric / cut / origin / care / wash code), and the CHAIN OF CUSTODY accordion
owns ALL delivery and returns information. Mobile-first: 2–4 short lines.

## Rules distilled from the audit (violations were the shoppers' worst moments)
R1. NO delivery/dispatch times in descriptions — custody owns them; the
    "9-16 days" line nearly killed sale 02 ("the AliExpress line").
R2. Fit words MUST agree with the cut metafield — "baggy, stacked" vs spec
    "OG straight" made the size-anxious buyer (03) distrust everything.
R3. Name the colour/wash IN TEXT — neither jeans PDP says "grey" or "blue"
    anywhere in words; on the near-black theme the wash is unreadable in
    photos (10); screen-reader users get no visual at all (16).
R4. No overclaims: every material fact must trace to the data table below.
    "Premium heavyweight cotton" on a 220gsm tee is an overclaim.
R5. No urgency, no fabricated content, no filler ("premium", "built for
    everyday wear" = words that could sit on any store).
R6. Plain-English facts; fiction stays in flavour (design law: the fiction
    stops where it would cost a sale).
R7. Don't duplicate the SPECIFICATION accordion line-for-line — the
    description's job is what the thing IS and looks/wears like, in voice.
R8. The brand's substitute for social proof is exact, self-consistent
    numbers — one wrong claim in a description spends that budget.

- 09 (gift buyer): what-you-get clarity (esp. the set and sock packs).
- 02/05 (sceptics): zero overclaim, zero filler; exactness IS the brand.

## Open questions for the council (do not dodge these)
Q-a: Should descriptions mention the set saving (drafts 10/11/13) when the set
     toggle UI already states it — reinforcement or duplication risk (the £10
     number would then live in three places that must never drift)?
Q-b: "3D embroidery on the chest" (draft 10) — placement is NOT in the data
     (photos suggest it; not verified). Keep, soften, or cut?
Q-c: Sock drafts claim colour placement ("black with blue detailing") from the
     titles alone — acceptable inference or overreach?
Q-d: crxst-rz-t-shirt has NO data — is keeping the current voice-only copy
     right, or should the council mandate a data-backed minimum (and flag the
     missing metafields to the owner instead of padding the copy)?
Q-e: Duffle draft is two sentences on an £18 product with two data points —
     enough, or does sparse-but-true beat padded?
Q-f: "Holds its shape all day" / "Heavy enough to hang straight" — fair
     texture inference from 14oz/500gsm, or R4 overclaim?
