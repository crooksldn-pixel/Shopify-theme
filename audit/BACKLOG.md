# BACKLOG.md — CROOKSLDN

Every finding, ranked by **severity ÷ effort**. The top five are what to do first.

**Severity:** `BLOCKS` = blocks a purchase or costs money outright · `HESITATION` = causes hesitation · `POLISH` = costs polish.
**Effort:** `minutes` · `hours` · `session` (half a day or more).
**Lens:** which persona or metric surfaced it, and whether it is a brand issue, a conversion issue, or both.

Theme paths refer to `origin/claude/crooksldn-theme-init-bnen7a@1b6bc4c`, which is byte-identical in structure to the staging theme. **Note:** items marked *admin* are live-on-save with no version control and no undo.

## Explicitly not recommended

- Adding trust badges, a reviews widget, countdown timers, stock counters or urgency messaging. Verified absent by measurement (`starRatings: 0`, `reviewBlocks: 0`, `urgency: false`) and deliberately so.
- Softening corners, warming the palette, or introducing a friendlier typeface.
- Reducing or removing the canvas board. It is measured at 60 fps, **0 fps** off-screen, on tab blur and under `prefers-reduced-motion`, and contributes **CLS 0** on the homepage across a 10 s throttled load.
- Reordering `templates/index.json` to lift the informant register above the lookbook — verified already the case. Lifting it further means moving it above `crooks_case_file`, which is what persona 5 stays for. Not worth the trade on current evidence.
- Rendering placeholder leaderboard scores.
