REFUND POLICY

Returns & Refunds
We want you in the right fit. If something's not right, we'll sort it.

Changed your mind or wrong size? You have 14 days from delivery to return or exchange any unworn item with tags on. Return postage is paid by you — that covers a change of mind, the wrong size, a swap, or any other reason of your own. There is no fee for a UK size swap itself, and we cover the postage sending the new size out to you. For returns please return to: Oairo Uk Office, Bourne end Business Park, Bourne End, Buckinghamshire, United Kingdom, SL8 5AS.

Faulty or wrong item? That's on us. Message us within 14 days and we'll refund or replace it in full, and we cover the postage both ways. A quick photo helps us fix it fast.

How: email crooksldn@gmail.com or DM @crooksldn with your order number. We reply within 1–2 working days; approved refunds land on your original payment method within 5–7 days.

Shipping: Free UK shipping over £20 (free Tracked 24 over £70). UK delivery 1–2 working days, international 7–14. Tracking sent by email.

Items marked final sale are clearly labelled and can't be returned. This doesn't affect your statutory rights.
