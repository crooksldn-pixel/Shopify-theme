# 04 — The set buyer, came for a crewneck and met a bundle toggle for the first time
**Device:** mobile (390x844) · **Goal:** buy the Charcoal Cellblock Crewneck (£50), M top / L bottoms · **Mood:** decided, mildly wary of upsells

### Step 1 — Landed on the homepage, got the cookie wall and the game popup
**Did:** Opened the site. Cookie consent banner first; tapped Accept. About a second later a full popup: "CROOKSLDN: THE GETAWAY — Crack the cuffs. 10% off your first order — code sent by text. Attempts unlimited. RUN IT / NOT NOW / One code per player." Tapped the × (observed only, did not play or give a number).
**Got:** Both overlays gone in two taps. (r04-01, r04-02, r04-03)
**Expected:** Some interruption — every small brand does this.
**Felt:** "Code sent by text? On a first visit that's forward. 'NOT NOW' is right there though, and it didn't nag me again."
**Next:** Find the crewneck.

### Step 2 — Homepage catalogue to the crewneck
**Did:** Tapped CATALOGUE (it anchors down the homepage to the product register). Scrolled to the card: "NO. 01 SWEATS — CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK CREWNECK — £50.00 — AVAILABLE". Tapped it.
**Got:** PDP. Big flat photo of the crewneck, £50.00, XS–XL size row, SIZE GUIDE, "Order before 18:00 and it ships today (Mon–Sat)". Note: nothing on the catalogue card mentioned a set. (r04-04, r04-05, r04-06)
**Expected:** Exactly this.
**Felt:** "Fine. £50, sizes look in stock, ships today. Easy."
**Next:** Pick M and go.

### Step 3 — Met the "Cop the full fit" control cold
**Did:** Scrolled to the buy area and found, between the dispatch line and the button, an unticked checkbox with a small thumbnail of shorts and one line: "Cop the full fit — add the matching Cellblock Shorts. Save £10."
**Got:** That one line is all the cold state gives: no price for the shorts, no £85, no ticked-by-default trickery. The £45 shorts price does appear further down in MORE FROM THIS DROP if you look. (r04-08)
**Expected:** I'd braced for a pre-ticked upsell. It isn't — it's opt-in and quiet.
**Felt:** "I get the gist: tick it, get the shorts too, save a tenner. What I don't know yet is what the total will be — I have to either trust them or scroll down to find the shorts price and do £50+£45−£10 myself. A stranger shouldn't need a calculator."
**Next:** Tick it and see.

### Step 4 — Ticked it; a second size grid appeared
**Did:** Tapped the checkbox label.
**Got:** A panel unfolded below: "CELLBLOCK SHORTS SIZE" with its own XS–XL row, a line saying "Pick your size first", and "FREE UK TRACKED 24 INCLUDED". The main button still said SELECT SIZE. (r04-09)
**Expected:** Something like this — two garments, two sizes.
**Felt:** "Now it's clear I'm getting BOTH pieces, each in its own size. Good. 'Free UK Tracked 24 included' on top of the tenner off — the deal is stacking up."
**Next:** Pick the shorts size.

### Step 5 — Tapped the shorts size first and thought it hadn't worked
**Did:** Tapped L in the shorts grid (bottoms first, that's how I think).
**Got:** L highlighted — but the line underneath still said "Pick your size first", and the big button still said SELECT SIZE. No price appeared. (r04-10)
**Expected:** Picking a size to register and something to acknowledge it.
**Felt:** "Did that not take? …Oh. 'Your size' means the CREWNECK size, up top. The message sits directly under the shorts grid I just used, so it reads like it's talking about the shorts. Five seconds of doubting my thumb."
**Next:** Pick the crewneck size.

### Step 6 — Picked M up top; the arithmetic did itself
**Did:** Tapped M in the main SIZE row.
**Got:** "IN STOCK" under the main grid, and in the set panel: "£95" struck through, "£85 for the set", CTA became "ADD THE FULL FIT — £85". Shorts L still selected. The sticky bar at the bottom mirrored it: "ADD THE FULL FIT — £85 / CHECKOUT NOW". (r04-11)
**Expected:** To have to verify the maths myself.
**Felt:** "There it is — £95 crossed out, £85, and the button repeats the number. £50+£45 is £95, so the £10 story checks out without me doing anything. Now I trust it."
**Next:** Add it.

### Step 7 — Added the full fit
**Did:** Tapped ADD THE FULL FIT — £85.
**Got:** Stayed on the page; a quiet line "> Added — 2 in bag View bag" appeared under the buttons and the header flipped to BAG [2]. No drawer, no popup. (r04-12, r04-13)
**Expected:** Some confirmation — got a subtle but findable one.
**Felt:** "'2 in bag' — for a heartbeat I thought I'd double-added the crewneck. Then: right, two garments. The set is one purchase but the counter counts pieces."
**Next:** Check the bag before checkout — always.

### Step 8 — The cart: exactly what I meant to buy
**Did:** Tapped BAG [2].
**Got:** Cart page. One line: "CELLBLOCK SET — CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK CREWNECK - M / CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK SHORTS - L — £85.00", qty 1. Above it a full shipping bar: "> Free Tracked 24 — unlocked" with ✓ TRACKED 48 FREE and ✓ TRACKED 24 FREE both ticked. A Discount accordion, Estimated total £85.00 GBP, "Pay in 3 interest-free instalments of £28.33 with Shop", "Duties and taxes included. Shipping is calculated at checkout." Check out button. Small things: the cart badge says "Cart 1" while the header says BAG [2] — piece-count vs line-count disagree; and the set's thumbnail shows only the crewneck, no shorts. (r04-14, r04-15)
**Expected:** Two lines, honestly — I half expected the "set" to arrive as separate crewneck + shorts and to have to check the discount applied.
**Felt:** "One line, both garments named with MY sizes, £85, shipping unlocked. That is precisely what I thought I was buying. 'Shipping is calculated at checkout' next to a bar shouting free is a small mixed message, but the bar's ticks win."
**Next:** I'd press Check out.

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Would buy — reached the cart with Cellblock Set (crewneck M, shorts L) at £85 and would press Check out; stopped there per audit rules.
**Total time:** ~5 minutes
**Worst moment:** Tapping the shorts size and reading "Pick your size first" directly beneath it — it means the crewneck size, but placed under the shorts grid it reads like my tap failed.
**Best moment:** The cart line — "CELLBLOCK SET" with both garments and both my sizes spelled out at £85, matching the struck-through £95 on the PDP. No surprises, arithmetic done for me.
**Would they come back?** Yes — the set control is opt-in, honest about the saving once sizes are picked, and the cart tells the truth.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Nothing stopped the purchase — but if the cold-state toggle line showed the set price up front ("£85 for both — save £10"), I'd have ticked it without the pause to hunt for the shorts price.
