# 14 — Trevor, 56, back a week later to finally get those £6 socks
**Device:** old Android, 360x800, congested 4G (slow4g throttle, CPU throttled 4x) · **Goal:** the white socks he abandoned last week when the site tried to charge him £12 for one pair — and this time he's watching that checkout like a hawk · **Mood:** sofa, about 10pm, kettle on. Expects the internet to be rubbish and the checkout to try it on again.

### Step 1 — Opened the site and watched it load
**Did:** Tapped his bookmark and sat there, tea in hand, counting in his head.
**Got:** By the 3-second mark the top of the page was already there and readable: FREE UK SHIPPING ticker, handcuffs logo, SHOP / SEARCH / BAG / MENU, "> 13 PRODUCTS AVAILABLE TO PURCHASE", the big CROOKSLDN — OWN THE STREETS™ title, purple CATALOGUE button — and the first three WORLD OF CROOKS photos were in as well. Typewriter letters already in their proper font, no substitute text flashing. At 5 seconds: same page, a photo or two more. At 10 seconds: same page again, more photos quietly filled in further down. He checked deliberately this time: the header, title and photo frames sat at exactly the same spot at 3, 5 and 10 seconds. Nothing jumped. Photos kept trickling in below the fold for a good half minute, but always into dark frames — never a white hole. (r14-01-home-3s.png, r14-02-home-5s.png, r14-03-home-10s.png, r14-06-home-30s-settled.png)
**Expected:** The usual: a spinner, then everything shoving everything else around as pictures land.
**Felt:** "Still quick. Quicker than the news site." Three seconds to readable on his connection is, in his experience, close to a miracle.
**Next:** continued

### Step 2 — The cookie box, and a well-behaved popup
**Did:** Read the front page with the COOKIE CONSENT box sat over the bottom half of the screen (it slid up at about the 5–6 second mark). He ignored it for a long while on purpose this time — last week his son had told him "there's a game on that site, dad" and he'd never seen it.
**Got:** As long as the cookie box sat unanswered, no game, no popup, nothing ambushed him — a full half-minute, nothing. The moment he tapped Accept, about a second later, the screen faded to black and "CROOKSLDN: THE GETAWAY" came up — all the words already there the instant it appeared, no empty box filling in later: "Crack the cuffs. 10% off your first order — code sent by text. Attempts unlimited." Two buttons: RUN IT and NOT NOW. Small print: "One code per player." (r14-04-popup-first-sighting.png, r14-07-popup-full.png)
**Expected:** From what his son said, some flashy thing shoved in his face while he was still reading. And on his internet, popups usually arrive as an empty white rectangle that loads its words five seconds later.
**Felt:** "At least it waited its turn." The popup queuing politely behind the cookie box — and arriving with its text already on — genuinely surprised him. "Code sent by text" though — that means giving them his number. Hmm.
**Next:** curiosity won — tapped RUN IT

### Step 3 — Ran his son's game, and the game had him on
**Did:** Tapped RUN IT.
**Got:** A black screen saying only "OPENING THE LOCK-UP…" for a couple of seconds (r14-31-iframe-intro.png), then a panel slid up from the bottom: "CRACK THE CUFFS. 10% off your first order if you do. Three tumblers. Tap each one at the right moment." RUN IT / NOT NOW again — the whole top three-quarters of the screen just black void with a second × floating up in the corner. Small print now added "Code expires in 20 minutes." He tapped RUN IT a second time. The game: "TAP TO STOP", a purple bar draining, three tumbler tiles all spinning the same digit in step — 7, 5, 1, 8… changing every half second on his phone. He tapped the tumblers, repeatedly, maybe ten taps over ten seconds. The counter said "0/3 LOCKED" the entire time — not one tap ever locked a tumbler that he could see. Then, right as the bar emptied, everything flashed at once: "CUFFS OPEN — 3/3 LOCKED — You cracked the cuffs. Cutting your code." — and then, one second later: "CUFFS HOLD. Run it again — attempts are unlimited. Or skip the game — join the register on the site for first word of every drop." No code. No text message screen. Nothing. (r14-26-second-sheet.png, r14-32-game-t2.png)
**Expected:** Tap, tumbler stops, three stops, code. Like a fruit machine.
**Felt:** "It told me I'd cracked it, then told me I hadn't. In the same breath." On his phone the taps simply didn't register — and he genuinely couldn't tell you what "the right moment" was supposed to be, because nothing on screen said. It felt like the machine was rigged. He wasn't giving them his phone number for a rematch.
**Next:** tapped the "join the register" line half by accident — game vanished and dumped him at a "REGISTER AS INFORMANT" email box on the site (r14-34-game-end.png). He did not fill it in. On to the socks.

### Step 4 — The socks page, no waiting
**Did:** Went to the white MOTIONTEC™ socks.
**Got:** Page complete inside 3 seconds — photo of the socks in place, "PRODUCT 11 / 13", £6.00, quantity choices 1pc / 3pc / 6pc / 12pc, and the bar pinned at the bottom reading "SELECT QUANTITY … CHECKOUT NOW" until he tapped 1pc, at which point it read "£6.00 · 1pc — ADD TO BAG — CHECKOUT NOW". Identical at 5 and 10 seconds — it was simply done. (r14-12-pdp-3s.png, r14-21-pdp-1pc-selected.png)
**Expected:** Product pages are usually the slow ones on his phone.
**Felt:** "Fastest page on the site, again." Also quietly pleased it makes you pick a quantity before it lets you buy — no accidents that way.
**Next:** continued

### Step 5 — Added to bag — and the site actually answered him
**Did:** Tapped ADD TO BAG. Once. Then held his thumb OFF the screen, deliberately — last week this button's silence nearly had him double-tapping.
**Got:** The button flicked to "ADDING…" essentially instantly, and inside a couple of seconds the bottom bar read "> Added — 1 in bag" with the BAG counter up top ticking [0] → [1]. All of it happening right where his thumb was. (r14-22-after-atb.png)
**Expected:** Last week's silence — nothing near the button, counter ticking over a beat and a half later, him hovering wondering whether to press again.
**Felt:** "Well, that's sorted then." Said it out loud. The exact thing that nearly cost him double last time now answers before his thumb's even lifted.
**Next:** the moment of truth

### Step 6 — CHECKOUT NOW, with his eyes on the total
**Did:** Tapped CHECKOUT NOW in the bottom bar. First tap. Started counting.
**Got:** About three seconds and the checkout was up — no dead half-minute, no second tap needed. White page, handcuffs logo, "Order summary — £6.00". Six pounds. One pair. The pair he added, and nothing else snuck in on top. The express-payment buttons at the top were three empty grey slabs that never drew themselves on his connection, but the form below — Contact, Delivery, United Kingdom — was all there and ready. He stopped at the door, as agreed with himself: it's 10pm and the point was to check the total. (r14-24-checkout-landing.png)
**Expected:** £12. Honestly, he expected £12 again, and had a small speech prepared for his son.
**Felt:** "£6.00. There it is." The two things that beat him last week — the dead button and the phantom second pair — both gone. Mild snort at the grey slabs where Apple Pay or whatever should be, but that's cosmetic.
**Next:** done — he'll finish it tomorrow with his card in daylight, entirely satisfied the till isn't fiddling him

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Didn't complete payment (audit stops at the checkout door regardless) — but this time the door was open and the price was right: one tap on CHECKOUT NOW, ~3 seconds, £6.00 for exactly the one pair he added. Last week's double-add did not recur.
**Total time:** ~11 minutes
**Worst moment:** The game. His taps never locked a single tumbler on his old phone, no clue what "the right moment" meant, and then it flashed "CUFFS OPEN — you cracked the cuffs. Cutting your code" immediately followed by "CUFFS HOLD. Run it again" — told him he'd won and lost in the same second, and never showed any code.
**Best moment:** Tapping CHECKOUT NOW once and seeing £6.00 three seconds later. Honourable mention: ADD TO BAG answering "ADDING… > Added — 1 in bag" before his thumb lifted — the exact silence that caused last week's fiasco, fixed.
**Would they come back?** Yes — and this time he'd actually finish the order. Browsing was painless again (readable at 3 seconds, nothing ever jumped, no white boxes), and the till now behaves. He will not be playing the game again though.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Nothing stopped the purchase this time — so the one thing left: make the tumbler game register taps on a slow phone (and never say "you cracked it" and "run it again" in the same breath). It's the only part of the site that still treats his old Android as an afterthought.
