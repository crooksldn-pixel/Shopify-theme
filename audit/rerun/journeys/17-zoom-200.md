# 17 — Marjorie, 61, low vision, browses everything at 200% zoom
**Device:** Desktop 1440x900 at 200% browser zoom (effective viewport 720x450) · **Goal:** Buy one warm crewneck, start to finish, without anything falling off my screen · **Mood:** Patient but braced — half the internet breaks at 200%

### Step 1 — Opened the site
**Did:** Loaded the homepage at my usual zoom.
**Got:** Site loads clean. No sideways scrollbar — first thing I always check. Then a COOKIE CONSENT panel rose over the bottom half of my screen — 47% of my viewport, measured feeling of "the site is behind bars". All four buttons (Manage preferences / Accept / Decline, plus a Privacy Policy link) were on-screen though. (r17-01)
**Expected:** A cookie banner; they're all enormous at my zoom.
**Felt:** "Half my window gone before I've seen a single jumper. But at least nothing's cut off."
**Next:** Accept and move on.

### Step 2 — The Getaway popup, and does it fit my little window
**Did:** Accepted cookies. About a second later (30 seconds on my first visit — it varies) a full-screen takeover: "CROOKSLDN: THE GETAWAY — Crack the cuffs. 10% off your first order — code sent by text. Attempts unlimited." RUN IT / NOT NOW. (r17-02, r17-03)
**Got:** Credit where due: the whole card FITS my 450px-tall window. Title, copy, both buttons, the small print — all on-screen, nothing to scroll (the overlay technically scrolls 6px; you'd never notice). There's a big × floating at the TOP-LEFT over the shop's logo, separate from the card — took me a moment to work out it belonged to the popup and not the site.
**Expected:** Buttons pushed below my fold. Pleasantly wrong.
**Felt:** "It fits. I'm almost suspicious."
**Next:** I like a game. RUN IT.

### Step 3 — The game itself, at 200%
**Did:** Tapped RUN IT. A second screen (an embedded frame, 520x440 — also fits my window exactly): "CRACK THE CUFFS." — the popup can't decide if it's called GETAWAY or CUFFS — "Three tumblers. Click each one at the right moment", with its own RUN IT / NOT NOW gate. Ran it. (r17-04)
**Got:** The live game: three tumblers with countdown numbers and TAP buttons, "0/3 LOCKED — Click each tumbler before the bar drains." Every control fully on-screen at my zoom — the game is completely reachable and playable at 450px tall. (r17-05) My first go I tapped at my own speed — about a second and a half per tumbler — and lost: "CUFFS HOLD. Run it again — attempts are unlimited," with a RUN IT AGAIN button and a line offering the register instead. All visible, nothing clipped. (r17-06)
**Expected:** A reflex game to be miserable with my eyes. It was — the drain bar beat me — but the LOSING screen was kind and the retry was one tap.
**Felt:** "My reactions lost, not my screen. Fair enough. One more go."
**Next:** Run it again.

### Step 4 — I won — and the prize screen fell out the bottom of the card
**Did:** Second go, tapped as fast as I could find them. 3/3.
**Got:** "EVIDENCE Nº GTWY-99DY — 10% off. One use. Expires in 19:59." with a COPY CODE button — the code is given on screen now, no text message required, the phone number is only an optional "Want it kept on file?" extra. Good. BUT: at my window height the card overflows — the phone box (07XXXXXXXXX) is sliced in half at the card's bottom edge, and below it FILE IT and the polite decline "No need — I've got it" are ENTIRELY off-card (measured: content runs to 585px in a 440px frame). (r17-08) There's a hidden inner scroll area only 125px tall that holds them — my mouse wheel over the middle of the card did NOTHING (r17-10); only tabbing with the keyboard dragged them into view (r17-11). No scrollbar, no cue that more exists.
**Expected:** The prize screen to fit like every other screen had.
**Felt:** "I can see my code, so I'm happy — but if I'd WANTED to give them my number, or wanted the 'no thanks' button, I'd have sworn the card just... ended. A half-swallowed text box is the only hint."
**Next:** Copy the code in my head — GTWY-99DY, 20 minutes, tick tock — close with the ×, and shop. (I never gave a phone number.)

### Step 5 — Homepage and catalogue at 200%
**Did:** Scrolled the whole homepage, then hit CATALOGUE.
**Got:** Honest layout at my zoom: no horizontal scrolling anywhere on the page, all the way down (checked at six scroll depths). The "WORLD OF CROOKS" evidence film-strip pokes ~50px past my right edge but it's a scrolling carousel, not breakage. (r17-13-home-band2) Catalogue: FLAT / ON MODEL and ALL / DENIM / SWEATS / T-SHIRT / ACCESSORIES toggles all fit on one line, big grid cards two-up. Tidy. (r17-14) One tax: the ticker plus header stay pinned (~89px) so a fifth of my window is furniture while I scroll.
**Expected:** Overlapping toggles, clipped cards. None.
**Felt:** "Someone tested this narrow. It behaves like a good phone site, which at 200% is exactly what I need."
**Next:** The charcoal crewneck, NO. 01.

### Step 6 — Product page and THE table
**Did:** Opened CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK CREWNECK (£50). Scrolled to the size row, pressed SIZE GUIDE. (r17-16, r17-17)
**Got:** The measurements table in full: SIZE / CHEST / LENGTH / SHOULDER / SLEEVE, XS–XL, "TRUE TO SIZE. THESE ARE THE GARMENT'S OWN FULL MEASUREMENTS." At my zoom the WHOLE table fits — all five columns including SLEEVE on-screen at once, big readable cells, no sideways swipe needed (table is 646px in my 720px window). (r17-18) The CM/IN toggle flips the whole table to inches and back, instantly. (r17-19) For the record I also pinched my window narrower to see how it degrades: the table sits in its own scroll container (it scrolls WITHIN itself; the sleeve column comes into reach by scrolling the table, and the page itself never grows a horizontal scrollbar even at 390px). The only criticism: nothing tells you that container scrolls — no scrollbar cue — same trick as the popup's hidden scroll area.
**Expected:** The table to be the thing that broke — tables always are.
**Felt:** "This table is the best thing on the site. I can actually READ it, every column, my zoom, no games."
**Next:** M. Add to bag.

### Step 7 — Size, add, and the sticky bar
**Did:** Picked M, scrolled down to ADD TO BAG (it sits one screen below the size grid at my height — not sticky itself), pressed it.
**Got:** "> Added — 1 in bag" written right under the button, plus a "View bag" link, bag badge flipped to [1], and a sticky bottom bar appeared: product name, "Added — 1 in bag", ADD TO BAG and CHECKOUT NOW. (r17-23) No drawer flying out — fine by me. Also spotted a cross-sell tickbox: "Cop the full fit — add the matching Cellblock Shorts. Save £10." Between pinned top (89px) and sticky bottom (~70px) about a THIRD of my 450px window is now chrome.
**Expected:** Some confirmation. Got a clear one.
**Felt:** "It told me plainly, in words, where I could see them. More sites should."
**Next:** The bag, then that code — clock's running.

### Step 8 — Cart, and the code "works"
**Did:** Opened the cart. Found a collapsed "Discount" disclosure under the total, opened it, typed GTWY-99DY, pressed Apply.
**Got:** Cart is clean at my zoom: shipping progress bar ("£30.00 to free Tracked 24", TRACKED 48 FREE already ticked), the crewneck, quantity buttons, Remove — nothing clipped, no sideways scroll. (r17-24) And the code took: "GTWY-99DY -£5.00", Estimated total £45.00. Exactly the promised 10%. (r17-25f)
**Expected:** A fiddly code box. It was fine.
**Felt:** "£45. The silly handcuff game actually paid out. I'm charmed."
**Next:** Check out — the button says "Check out", down below the totals.

### Step 9 — Checkout: my £45 became £50 and my code became worthless
**Did:** Pressed Check out. Landed on the real checkout (crooksldn.com). Opened the Order summary to check my £45.
**Got:** "Total price £50.00." Subtotal £50.00. Total GBP £50.00. My discount — the one the cart had just shown me in black and white — is GONE; no GTWY line anywhere. (r17-29) So I typed GTWY-99DY into the checkout's own Discount code box and pressed Apply: red border, "Enter a valid discount code." (r17-30) I wondered if my 20 minutes had lapsed while I shopped — plausible at my speed, and its own cruelty — so afterwards I tested it properly: won a FRESH code (GTWY-ZKP9), applied it at the cart (again "-£5.00, £45.00"), went straight to checkout within a minute — same result: £50.00, "Enter a valid discount code." (r17-31, r17-32) The game's codes do not exist as far as the till is concerned. The cart's -£5.00 is theatre.
**Expected:** £45.00 at checkout. Obviously.
**Felt:** "That's not a bug, that's a broken promise. The cart LIED to me. If I'd had my card out I'd have paid £5 more than the shop itself told me, or spent ten minutes retyping a code that was never real."
**Next:** I stopped at the landing page — I wasn't paying £50 for a £45 jumper on principle. (Audit note: hard stop before any personal details regardless.)

### Step 10 — Aftermath
**Did:** Went back for a last look. The Getaway popup greeted me AGAIN — it reopens on every fresh visit, even though I'd played, won, and closed it.
**Got:** Same full-screen takeover, third time today.
**Expected:** Won once, "one code per player" — surely it remembers me.
**Felt:** "You've cuffed me twice already, dear."
**Next:** Close the tab.

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Didn't. Mechanically I could have — every page, form and button was reachable at 200% zoom, which is rarer than it should be — but the checkout wanted £50 for a bag the cart priced at £45, and the "prize" code was refused at the only place it matters. I wasn't rewarding that with my card.
**Total time:** ~34 minutes (a good ten of them on the code that never worked).
**Worst moment:** Watching "Estimated total £45.00" turn into "Total £50.00 / Enter a valid discount code" — twice, once with a code seconds old.
**Best moment:** The measurements table at 200%: all five columns, readable cells, CM/IN toggle, zero sideways scrolling. The single most zoom-friendly size guide I've met.
**Would they come back?** For the clothes and the layout, yes — the site itself is genuinely usable at 200%. But not until the discount game either pays out at checkout or stops promising 10%.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Make the Getaway codes real Shopify discount codes so cart and checkout agree — I'd have paid £45 on the spot.
