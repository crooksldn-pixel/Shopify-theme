# 09 — Denise, buying her nephew's 16th-birthday present without knowing his size
**Device:** Mobile (390x844, no throttle) · **Goal:** Find a gift for a 16-year-old whose size I don't know — ideally a gift card; failing that, something safe. Check size help, wrapping, and what happens if it's wrong. · **Mood:** Wednesday evening, mildly dutiful. His birthday is a week Saturday. He follows this brand on TikTok, so here I am.

### Step 1 — Arrived on the homepage
**Did:** Opened the site, waited for it to settle.
**Got:** Dark, terminal-looking shop — handcuffs logo, "13 PRODUCTS AVAILABLE TO PURCHASE", big CATALOGUE button. A cookie banner covered the entire bottom half of my phone. (r09-01)
**Expected:** A shop. Got a shop, plus a wall of cookie text.
**Felt:** "It's very… police evidence themed. He'd love it. I feel old."
**Next:** Tapped Accept to make the banner go away.

### Step 2 — The popup game got me
**Did:** Scrolled the homepage for ten seconds, then a full-screen popup slid up: "CROOKSLDN: THE GETAWAY — Crack the cuffs. 10% off your first order — code sent by text." RUN IT / NOT NOW. (r09-03)
**Got:** I nearly closed it — "code sent by text" means giving a shop my phone number, and I don't do that. But 10% off a £50 gift is £5, so I peeked: RUN IT led to an explainer — three tumblers, tap each at the right moment, unlimited attempts, code expires in 20 minutes. (r09-04)
**Expected:** A form demanding my number before anything happened.
**Felt:** "If it asks for my number before showing me anything, I'm out."
**Next:** Played it.

### Step 3 — Played the tumbler game, fat-fingered the close button, won anyway
**Did:** Tapped RUN IT. The game (three tumblers, a draining bar, "0/3 LOCKED") sits at the bottom of the screen — and the first thing my thumb hit was the close X sitting right beside it, which killed the whole thing. (r09-11) The popup came back on my next page anyway, so I went again and tapped the three tumblers.
**Got:** A win screen: "EVIDENCE Nº GTWY-JPAA — 10% off. One use. Expires in 19:58 — COPY CODE." The phone number bit is OPTIONAL — "Want it kept on file? Phone number goes in the evidence log" with a FILE IT button and a "No need — I've got it" opt-out. I also caught a flash of "CODE EXPIRED" before the fresh code drew in. (r09-15)
**Expected:** From the teaser, I expected the code held hostage behind an SMS. It isn't — the code is right there on screen and the phone ask is a polite opt-in afterwards.
**Felt:** "That was actually fun. But why did the popup TELL me it needed my number when it doesn't? I nearly didn't play. And now I'm on a 20-minute clock and I don't even know his size."
**Next:** Wrote the code on the back of an envelope. Catalogue.

### Step 4 — Browsed the catalogue for gift material
**Did:** Opened CATALOGUE. 13 items: crewnecks, shorts, jeans, jorts, tees, sweats, socks, a duffle. (r09-16)
**Got:** Clear prices, clear stock notes ("2 OF 5 SIZES LEFT"). The CHARCOAL CELLBLOCK CREWNECK at £50 looked the safest — every teenager lives in one. No gift card tile anywhere in the grid.
**Expected:** Somewhere between hoodie chaos and a gift-card tile at the end. No gift card.
**Felt:** "A grey jumper. Safe. Now — what size is a lanky fifteen-almost-sixteen year old?"
**Next:** Opened the crewneck.

### Step 5 — Found an actual size guide on the product page
**Did:** On the crewneck page: sizes XS–XL and, right next to them, a SIZE GUIDE button. Tapped it.
**Got:** It scrolled me to an opened MEASUREMENTS section: a proper table — chest, length, shoulder, sleeve for every size, "TRUE TO SIZE. THESE ARE THE GARMENT'S OWN FULL MEASUREMENTS." — with a CM/IN toggle that works. (r09-18, r09-20, r09-21) One niggle: the sleeve column runs off the right edge of my phone and needs a sideways swipe to read.
**Expected:** Honestly? Nothing. Streetwear sites never have this. I was braced for "oversized fit lol".
**Felt:** "Oh, this is genuinely good. I could screenshot this to his mum and ask what his chest is. But it tells me what the JUMPER measures, not what a 5'9 sixteen-year-old takes — I still have to guess the mapping myself."
**Next:** Checked what happens if I guess wrong.

### Step 6 — Read the returns and exchange story
**Did:** Opened CHAIN OF CUSTODY — SHIPPING & RETURNS on the product page, then the FAQ ("HOW DO I KNOW WHAT SIZE TO BUY?", "DO YOU DO EXCHANGES?") and the REFUNDS policy page. (r09-22, r09-27, r09-30)
**Got:** 14 days from delivery to report, 14 more to post back, unworn with tags. Exchanges: yes — you pay return postage, the size swap itself is free and THEY pay postage sending the new size out. FAQ sizing advice: "compare them straight against something you already own", and if a piece isn't measured yet, "message us and we will measure it for you".
**Expected:** A grudging returns page. This is better than expected — a free UK size swap is exactly the gift-buyer's safety net.
**Felt:** "'Compare against something you already own' — I own none of his clothes, that advice is useless to a gift buyer. But the free size swap changes the maths: a wrong guess costs him a £3 stamp, not the gift. What's missing is any mention of GIFTS — if I bought this a month before his birthday, the 14 days would be gone before he opened the box. Buying nine days ahead, I just squeak in."
**Next:** One last hunt for the gift card.

### Step 7 — Hunted for a gift card and gift wrapping. There are none.
**Did:** Searched "gift card". Checked the menu drawer, the footer, the FAQ for "gift", "wrap", "voucher".
**Got:** Search: "NO PRODUCTS MATCH THAT. IF YOU WERE AFTER AN ANSWER RATHER THAN A PIECE, THE LINKS ABOVE HAVE IT." — except there are no links above on the results page; the handy direct links (returns / tracking / FAQ) only show before you search. (r09-25) Menu: shop links, tracking, questions, terms, contact, the game. FAQ: zero hits for gift, wrap, voucher. Nothing in the cart either, later — no gift note, no wrap option.
**Expected:** Every shop has a gift card. This one doesn't. Nor wrapping, nor a gift note, nor a gift receipt.
**Felt:** "You're a small brand selling to teenagers whose aunties have the money — and you won't sell me a £50 voucher? The only gifty thing on the whole site is the homepage brag that orders ship 'sealed, tagged and numbered' in a PROPERTY BAG — which, to be fair, does look like a present."
**Next:** Decision time: guess a size, buy socks, or leave.

### Step 8 — Decided: guess M, soften the risk
**Did:** Weighed it. Socks-only (£6–£12) feels cheap for a 16th. Leaving means starting over on another site. The measurements table + "true to size" + free size swap made guessing survivable. Picked the crewneck in M — the middle of five sizes, teenage-boy default — and added the white/red MOTIONTEC socks (1pc, £6) so there's a second thing to unwrap.
**Got:** "Added — 1 in bag" toast for the crewneck; the socks page sells by quantity (1pc/3pc/6pc/12pc), no size to pick — good. Bag [2], £56, over the £30 free-shipping bar. (r09-31 … r09-35)
**Expected:** This is roughly what I'd do in real life — M, and keep the receipt.
**Felt:** "M. If it's wrong, the swap's free. Don't overthink it, Denise."
**Next:** Cart, and try that code — it must be past its 20 minutes by now.

### Step 9 — The code worked in the cart… then vanished at checkout
**Did:** In the cart, found a little "Discount" disclosure, typed GTWY-JPAA, applied. Then tapped Check out.
**Got:** Cart: "GTWY-JPAA −£5.60", estimated total £50.40 — the "expires in 20 minutes" clock turned out to be a bluff, it was well past that. (r09-37) But the checkout page said Order summary: £56.00. No discount line. The £5.60 evaporated between cart and checkout; there's a separate "Discount code" box at checkout where I'd have to dig the code out and type it AGAIN. (r09-38, r09-39) Also noted: "Keep me updated" marketing tickbox comes pre-ticked, and the game popup had kept re-appearing on pages throughout — at one point it swallowed my tap on the QUESTIONS link.
**Expected:** The price I saw in the cart.
**Felt:** "There it is. The one moment this site made me feel like a mug. If I hadn't been paying attention I'd have paid the £5.60 back without knowing."
**Next:** Stopped at the checkout form (this was an audit — no real order). In real life: re-enter the code, grumble, pay.

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Bought (in spirit) — Charcoal Cellblock Crewneck in a guessed M (£50) + white/red socks (£6); reached the checkout landing and stopped there per audit rules. The 10% code applied in cart (£50.40) but was lost on the way to checkout (£56.00).
**Total time:** ~24 minutes
**Worst moment:** Realising at checkout that the discount the cart had confirmed was silently gone — after already discovering there's no gift card, no wrapping, no gift note anywhere.
**Best moment:** Tapping SIZE GUIDE and getting a real measurements table with a cm/in toggle, backed by a free UK size swap — it turned "I don't know his size" from a dealbreaker into a manageable guess.
**Would they come back?** Cautiously — if the M fits or the swap is as painless as promised. A gift card would have made me a repeat customer on the spot; every birthday, done in two minutes.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Sell a gift card. Failing that, a gift note/receipt with an extended exchange window for gifts — the 14-days-from-delivery clock punishes anyone buying ahead of a birthday.

## What exists vs what is missing (gift-buyer checklist)
- Size guide: EXISTS and is good — per-size garment measurements (chest/length/shoulder/sleeve), CM/IN toggle, "true to size" note, reachable via a SIZE GUIDE button beside the size picker. Gaps: no body-measurement/height mapping (it describes the garment, not the wearer), sleeve column clips off-screen on a 390px phone, and the FAQ's "compare against something you already own" advice is useless when you own nothing of the recipient's.
- Gift card: MISSING. Search "gift card" = no results; not in menu, footer, FAQ, or catalogue.
- Gift wrapping / gift note / gift receipt: MISSING everywhere (PDP, cart, checkout). Closest thing: the "sealed, tagged and numbered" property-bag packaging story on the homepage.
- Exchange policy: EXISTS and is genuinely gift-friendly in one respect — UK size swaps are free (buyer pays return postage; store pays outbound on the replacement). But: 14 days from delivery, no gift extension, refunds to the original payer only, started by email/DM rather than a form on the PDP.
