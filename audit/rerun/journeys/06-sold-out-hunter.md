# 06 — Dre, chasing the grey convict sweats in M and finding them gone
**Device:** mobile (390x844) · **Goal:** get the V2 Baggies (now GREY CONVICT SWEATS) in size M, via the homepage catalogue · **Mood:** keen, a bit resigned — I already suspect my size is gone

### Step 1 — Landed on the homepage, got greeted twice
**Did:** Opened crooksldn on my phone. Big cookie banner first — hit Decline. Kept scrolling, then about six seconds in a full-screen popup slid up: "CROOKSLDN: THE GETAWAY — Crack the cuffs. 10% off your first order — code sent by text."
**Got:** Two interruptions before I saw a single product. The popup is a huge dark sheet with a stray × in the top-left corner and the pitch panel low on the screen.
**Expected:** To be looking at clothes by now.
**Felt:** "10% off though. Code by text? Hmm. Let me see what this is."
**Next:** Tapped RUN IT out of curiosity.

### Step 2 — Tapped RUN IT and got… the same pitch again
**Did:** Tapped the purple RUN IT button.
**Got:** A second panel: "CRACK THE CUFFS. Three tumblers. Tap each one at the right moment." — with ANOTHER RUN IT button, plus small print: "One code per player. Attempts unlimited. Code expires in 20 minutes. (this drop closes 15.09)". Half the screen above it was empty black.
**Expected:** The game to start. Instead it felt like the button hadn't worked and it was re-pitching me.
**Felt:** "Didn't I just press run it? It wants my phone number for the code anyway — not doing that for 10%. Also — 'this drop closes 15.09'? So drops close. Noted." Closed it. (I never gave it a number.)
**Next:** Down to the catalogue.

### Step 3 — The catalogue card warned me before I clicked
**Did:** Scrolled the 13-item catalogue looking for the baggies. Found card NO. 09, SWEATS — "GREY CONVICT SWEATS £60.00" (that's the V2 Baggies, the photo is the same grey pair).
**Got:** Under the price, in purple: "2 OF 5 SIZES LEFT". Every other card just says "AVAILABLE". So the card did tell me stock was thin before I tapped — but not WHICH two sizes survived.
**Expected:** Exactly this kind of warning, credit to them. I braced for bad news.
**Felt:** "2 of 5. I'm an M. Odds are bad — M always dies first."
**Next:** Tapped the card.

### Step 4 — The size row confirmed it: M is dead
**Did:** PDP loaded — GREY CONVICT SWEATS £60.00. Scrolled to SIZE.
**Got:** XS and S in solid boxes; M, L, XL in dashed boxes with a line through the letter. So it's not "2 left" ambiguity — the grid shows exactly who's dead. I tapped M anyway; first tap didn't take (page was still settling), second tap lit it purple and red text appeared: "SIZE M IS SOLD OUT". The buy button flipped to a grey disabled SOLD OUT, the sticky bar at the bottom now says "£60.00 · M" with SOLD OUT next to a dimmed CHECKOUT NOW. I still tried tapping that CHECKOUT NOW — dead, properly disabled, nothing happened.
**Expected:** This. Still stings.
**Felt:** "Knew it. At least it tells me straight instead of letting me add it and failing at checkout."
**Next:** A red-bordered box appeared under the button: "TELL ME WHEN THIS SIZE IS BACK".

### Step 5 — Signed up to be told when it's back
**Did:** Read the notify box. It is one line — "TELL ME WHEN THIS SIZE IS BACK", an email field, a NOTIFY ME button. Nothing else. No "we'll email you within…", no "one email, then we delete it", nothing about whether restocks even happen. Typed audit-restock@example.com and hit NOTIFY ME.
**Got:** For two-three seconds: nothing. Button unchanged, no spinner. Then the page greyed out and a captcha appeared — a cartoon puzzle: "Drag ONE animal to the matching silhouette", a duck, a crow and a cat next to a prison-window photo, with Skip and refresh buttons.
**Expected:** A tick and "you're on the list".
**Felt:** "A children's puzzle? On this store? And nobody warned me — I thought the form had frozen until this thing appeared." The cutesy hCaptcha art on top of the prison-goth page is genuinely funny for the wrong reasons.
**Next:** (Audit rule: don't solve captchas.) I pressed back/Escape instead — treating it as the shopper who can't be bothered.

### Step 6 — Dismissed the captcha and got… silence
**Did:** Escaped out of the captcha.
**Got:** Back to the page exactly as before. My email still sits in the field. No error, no "not sent", no "finish the puzzle to sign up". Nothing anywhere says whether I'm on the list. (I'm not — the form never went through.)
**Expected:** SOMETHING. Even "signup cancelled".
**Felt:** "So am I signed up or not?" This is the trap: anyone who closes that puzzle walks away believing they'll get an email that will never come. I trust it maybe 30%.
**Next:** Do what I always do when a form feels flaky — screenshot the page for my own records, and find their Instagram. There it is in the footer: @crooksldn. Followed. That's my real restock alert.

### Step 7 — Hunted for a straight answer: will this EVER come back?
**Did:** Opened all four PDP accordions (CHAIN OF CUSTODY — SHIPPING & RETURNS, SPECIFICATION, ITEM DESCRIPTION, MEASUREMENTS). Then the FAQ page. Then skimmed the terms page because the FAQ pointed there.
**Got:** Nothing. The description just says "V2 Baggies — wide, full-length sweats in 500gsm cotton" (still the old name, mind — the title says GREY CONVICT SWEATS, the description says V2 Baggies). The FAQ has sections for delivery, sizing, returns, payment — not one question about restocks, drops or sold-out sizes. Terms mention drops only in passing ("After a drop, allow up to two working days…", "Drops are for people, not scripts"). The ONLY hint that stock is time-boxed is that popup's small print — "(this drop closes 15.09)" — which I'd never have seen if I hadn't poked the game.
**Expected:** One FAQ line: "Do you restock?" — yes or no. If this brand does short runs that never return, the form's own words — "when this size is BACK" — are quietly promising something that may never happen.
**Felt:** "They'll tell me the sweats are 500gsm and made in Portugal, but not whether waiting is pointless. If it's never coming back, tell me — I'd have bought the S for my girlfriend or the pink ones instead. Instead I'm parked on a maybe."
**Next:** Gave up. Followed the Instagram, kept my screenshot, left.

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Didn't buy. Tried to join the restock list for size M; the submission was intercepted by an hCaptcha puzzle which (per audit rules) I didn't solve — dismissing it left no confirmation and no error, so a real shopper in my seat likely believes they're on a list they never joined. Followed @crooksldn on Instagram as the fallback.
**Total time:** ~13 minutes
**Worst moment:** The silence after escaping the captcha — email still in the box, zero feedback, no idea if I'm signed up. Close second: discovering that nowhere on the whole site says whether anything ever restocks.
**Best moment:** The catalogue card saying "2 OF 5 SIZES LEFT" before I clicked, and the size grid's dashed-and-struck-through treatment — the bad news is delivered honestly and early.
**Would they come back?** For the brand, probably — via Instagram, not via this notify form. I don't trust the form to ever ping me.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** A single line under NOTIFY ME telling the truth about restocks — "Short runs. If it's gone it's gone; we'll email you only if this size returns before the drop closes 15.09" — plus a visible "You're on the list" confirmation (and a heads-up that a captcha is coming). With that, I'd have trusted the signup instead of leaving on a shrug.
