# 01 — Jay, cold Instagram click straight onto a product page, on the bus, wears L

**Device:** mobile 390x844, slow 4G throttle · **Goal:** in ~90 seconds: what is this, price, do they have L, is the shop real, when would it arrive · **Mood:** idle-curious, zero brand loyalty, thumb hovering over the back button

### Step 1 — Tapped the story link
**Did:** Tapped through from an Instagram story straight to /products/v2-baggies on bus data.
**Got:** Blank-ish beat, then at ~4–5s the whole page landed in one piece: big photo of grey sweatpants on a black terminal-looking design, name, price — and a cookie notice squatting on the bottom half of the screen.
**Expected:** Ten seconds of spinner and images popping in late, like most shop links off Instagram.
**Felt:** "Alright, that showed up quicker than I expected for the bus. Half the screen's a cookie thing though."
**Next:** Get rid of the banner.

### Step 2 — Cleared the cookie banner
**Did:** Tapped Accept (top button, biggest target, zero thought given).
**Got:** Banner gone instantly. Full page visible.
**Expected:** Exactly this.
**Felt:** "Standard. At least Accept and Decline are both right there, not buried."
**Next:** So what is this thing?

### Step 3 — Read the essentials off one screen
**Did:** Glanced top to mid-page without scrolling much.
**Got:** GREY CONVICT SWEATS, £60.00, "PRODUCT 09 / 13 — SWEATS", size row XS–XL right under the price. Top ticker: "FREE UK SHIPPING OVER £30 // ORDER BY 18:00 FOR SAME-DAY DISPATCH", and under the size grid "Order before 18:00 and it ships today (Mon–Sat) — Ordered now, leaves today".
**Expected:** To have to scroll and dig for price and shipping.
**Felt:** "Fair play — what it is, what it costs, when it ships, all in one eyeful. £60 for sweats is steep for a brand I've never heard of, but the page looks properly made, not a dropship template."
**Next:** Do they have L?

### Step 4 — Looked for L, tapped it anyway
**Did:** Looked at the size grid: XS and S in solid boxes, M, L and XL in dashed boxes with a line through the letter. Tapped L regardless, hoping.
**Got:** L highlighted purple, the buy button flipped to a dead SOLD OUT, and a red-outlined box appeared: "TELL ME WHEN THIS SIZE IS BACK" with an email field and NOTIFY ME.
**Expected:** Honestly, the strikethrough had already told me. Tapping was denial.
**Felt:** "Course it's gone. M and XL too — only the two smallest sizes left. The notify box is clear enough, but it doesn't say if this ever comes back or when I'd hear. I'm not typing my email on the bus for a brand I met forty seconds ago."
**Next:** Is this shop even legit, out of curiosity.

### Step 5 — Thirty-second legitimacy sniff
**Did:** Opened the "CHAIN OF CUSTODY — SHIPPING & RETURNS" accordion, then flicked to the footer.
**Got:** Numbered shipping story: dispatch same day before 18:00 Mon–Sat, Royal Mail Tracked, free UK over £30, UK 1–2 working days, tracking emailed. Footer has Questions, Terms, Shipping, Refunds, Track Order, Returns, Contact, Instagram, TikTok.
**Expected:** A ghost town footer — that's what fake shops look like.
**Felt:** "This reads like a real operation. Royal Mail, tracking, returns page, socials. The prison-paperwork theme is a bit much but it's committed. Would've trusted it with £60 if they had my size."
**Next:** One last look — those pink ones further down.

### Step 6 — Hopped to the pink pair hoping for an L
**Did:** Scrolled to "MORE FROM THIS DROP", tapped PINK CONVICT SWEATS £60. Card showed just name and price — no stock hint.
**Got:** ~5s load on bus data, then the same size grid: L dashed and struck through there too. M and XL had a tiny purple dot next to them — no idea what that meant.
**Expected:** A coin flip. Lost it.
**Felt:** "Two for two, my size is dead across the drop. The card could've told me before I burned the tap. And what's the little dot — low stock? Says nowhere."
**Next:** Nothing left for me here. Closed the tab, back to Instagram.

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Didn't buy, didn't leave an email — size L sold out on the landing product and the sister colour.
**Total time:** ~2 minutes (the pink detour pushed it past the 90 seconds).
**Worst moment:** Realising every size from M up was struck out, and the only offer left was "give us your email" with no word on whether restocks even happen.
**Best moment:** The first screen answering everything at once — name, £60, sizes, free shipping over £30, ships today if ordered by 18:00 — after a fully-formed ~5s load on throttled 4G. No popup ever interrupted either.
**Would they come back?** Only if the algorithm serves them again and something's in stock. Nothing was captured — no email, no follow, no bookmark — so the shop is relying on luck. 50/50 at best.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** An L in stock, obviously — failing that, a notify box that says whether/when restocks happen, and stock hints on the "MORE FROM THIS DROP" cards so the second dead end never happened.
