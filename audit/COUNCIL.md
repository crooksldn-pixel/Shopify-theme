## WHERE THE COUNCIL AGREES — unanimous

**1. The new work held, and it finished the trust story.** Search works end-to-end on first
measurement; TERMS states the return window and postage liability with zero placeholders; the
FAQ answers what waiting customers actually ask; all of it in-voice, no-JS-safe, accessible.
Zero of eight personas now abandon (3 → 1 → 0 across the audits). The sceptic — a leaver in
two consecutive audits — now stays. The fiction survived being made functional.

**2. One font mechanic owns every remaining theme defect.** CRX Mono's swap wraps the new
header row: 48 px drop at t≈1.9 s, CLS 0.325–0.391 on every mobile page, 0.59 on the cold-click
journey, and V2 BAGGIES' size row pushed to the fold. Ablation-proven; citations exact
(`crx-mono.css:7,14` swap; `crooks.css:397` min-height; `:431–434` wrap). This is the same
defect class that topped run 2 (VT323 meta row), prescribed the same fix (`size-adjust`
fallbacks), which appears **zero times** in the deployed CSS. **The Contrarian's structural
ruling is adopted:** font fallback metrics belong in the token layer, enforced the way
`border-radius: 0` is enforced at `crooks.css:101` — in code, not in review. Until then, every
new mono-set surface ships this bug again.

**3. The DENY flip is no longer chronic — it is a countdown.** Third audit with `CONTINUE` on
all three tees; nine days of live trade (22 orders) have sold variants down to 8–9 units. The
next sell-out either fires the notify capture the theme built, or sells phantom stock a third
time. Every advisor ranked it top-two; the Contrarian proposed the only enforcement mechanism
this checkbox has ever had: **no fourteenth theme commit until it flips.** Adopted as the
recommendation's framing.

**4. The email string.** `crooksldn@gmail.com.com` — undeliverable, on the shipping policy, on
the brand's best-ever trust surface. The theme's own footer default is already
`info@crooksldn.com` (`crooks-footer-log.liquid:123`); the Gmail is admin-entered content.
One find-and-replace completes the sceptic conversion three audits in the making.
