## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Bought (audit sense) — reached the checkout landing page with a £70 basket: USA Top S + Italy Track Pants S. But it's a consolation outfit; the grey Bird set he came to buy (0/10 and 0/10 variants) was impossible, and so was every other same-nation two-piece in his size — the store's ONLY same-size set in stock is Portugal in XS.
**Total time:** ~20 minutes, most of it doing stock-and-size reconciliation the store should have done for him.
**Worst moment:** Discovering the "Italy set" — the one set collection with two buyable products — cannot be purchased in a single size (polo XS/XL vs pants S), right after two other "Sets" collections ("Black And Grey Sets", "Denim Set") turned out to be beautiful empty pages, one of them ("Nations Shorts", also 0 products) linked from the main menu.
**Best moment:** The cart and checkout — instant drawer feedback, correct live maths on edits, taxes included, a branded professional checkout; and the small win that his hand-built USA-top/Italy-pants clash outfit genuinely looked good.
**Would they come back?** Grudgingly maybe — the product photography and the clash-kit aesthetic won him over — but only if an Instagram post shows the Bird line restocked; the site gave him no notify-me and no reason to check back on its own.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** Sell sets as sets — restock both halves of a nation in aligned sizes (or a "shop the full look" block on each PDP that only offers size-matched pairs). Dan arrived wanting to spend £62 on two items; the store made him fight for 20 minutes to give it £70, and a size-matched Bird restock would have taken £62 in about 90 seconds.
