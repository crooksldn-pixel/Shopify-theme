### Step 2 — Shop all: found the card, and it told me before I tapped
**Did:** Opened Shop all ("Showing 20 of 44 products", A–Z) and scrolled the grid to B.

Meanwhile in-stock cards (Argentina Shorts et al.) carry a black "+" quick-add button, so the grid genuinely distinguishes buyable from dead — credit where due, the card was honest with me BEFORE the click.
**Expected:** Honestly? Half expected this. But the badge on the card at least saved me a surprise.
**Felt:** "Okay. Sold out. But sold out sold out? Sometimes that badge just means one size is gone. I'm tapping in anyway — maybe M survived, maybe there's a pre-order."

### Step 3 — The PDP: ten sizes, ten strikethroughs, nothing for my money
**Did:** PDP loaded in ~4s. Read the buy box top to bottom, then tapped "M - IN HAND" and "M - PRE ORDER" anyway.
**Got:** SOLD OUT badge, £27.00 taxes included, 4 photos (front, back, one lifestyle, back again), shipping lines "In Hand Please Allow 5-8 Working Days / Pre Order - Please Allow 3-4 Weeks For Your Item" — and that is the ENTIRE product description. Then the size grid: ten chips, XS–XL "IN HAND" and XS–XL "PRE ORDER", every single one struck through, each announcing "VARIANT SOLD OUT OR UNAVAILABLE" to a screen reader. Tapping M did literally nothing — the selector stayed on "XS - IN HAND", the buy button stayed a disabled grey "Sold Out". Verified in the product data: 0 of 10 variants purchasable. And under the dead button: a bright, live-looking purple "Buy with shop" button and "MORE PAYMENT OPTIONS", on a product you cannot buy.
**Expected:** The store sells pre-orders as a concept — "3-4 weeks", fine, take my money. Or a restock date. Or anything.
**Felt:** "Even the PRE-ORDER is sold out. How is a pre-order sold out? You have a whole two-tier system for taking money for stuff you don't have yet, and I still can't give you £27. And why is there a purple buy button glowing under a dead one?"
**Next:** hesitated — started hunting (u06-06, u06-08, u06-09, u06-10)

### Step 4 — The hunt: any way at all to be told when it's back?
**Did:** Combed the PDP for a notify-me. Mid-hunt the newsletter popup slid over the bottom half of the screen. Then scrolled to the footer, then read About us and Contact.
**Got:** • On the PDP: no "notify me", no waitlist, no restock date, no back-in-stock anything — the only interactive things on a 0/10 product are a quantity stepper and dead buy buttons. • The popup: "Sign up for our newsletter — Stay informed about new collections and discounts" — collections and discounts, not restocks, and not "your size". Dismissed via NO THANKS. • Footer: a newsletter block — "Sign up to find out about future releases and promotions" — closest thing to a restock channel in the whole store; plus two unlabelled icons: Instagram and TikTok (@unfoundedstudios). • About us: "Every drop is intentional, focused on clean design, limited releases" — so scarcity is the brand, but nowhere does anyone explain how drops work, when restocks happen, or how to hear about them. • Contact page (menu only, not in footer): opening hours, a form (name, contact by email/phone/SMS, message) and info@unfoundedstudios.com — I *could* write "when are the grey joggers back in M?" like it's 2009. Observed, not submitted.
**Expected:** A "Notify me when back" button on the dead size. That's the whole ask. It's a solved problem on every Shopify store I shop at.
**Felt:** "So the machinery to email me exists — there's a popup BEGGING for my email — but the one email I'd actually opt into, 'M is back', isn't on offer. 'Future releases and promotions' is a maybe-sort-of proxy. Would I use it? …Honestly yes, grudgingly, it's the only lever in the shop — that and going back to TikTok to follow them, which is where I came from. Full circle."
**Next:** continued — checked alternatives before giving up (u06-08, u06-11, u06-12, u06-13)

**Got:** On the actual PDP, M is struck through — "VARIANT SOLD OUT OR UNAVAILABLE". Only S is buyable ("Only 3 left in stock. Order soon."). The card's "Available in 5 size" and its M chip count sizes that *exist*, not sizes you can buy. To its credit this PDP has a real size-chart image (waist/length in inches) — the joggers had nothing.
**Expected:** A card that shows me an "M - IN HAND" chip to have an M in hand.
**Felt:** "That's the second time this shop has shown me an M that doesn't exist. The sold-out cards are honest; the in-stock cards oversell. Backwards."

### Step 6 — Last sweep: is there ANY size M in this entire store?
**Did:** Searched "joggers" (13 results — all Bird joggers/shorts and nation track pants, every card either SOLD OUT-badged or "Available in 5 size" teasing). Then, being thorough, checked every one of the ~12 buyable products' actual availability.
**Got:** The store's entire purchasable stock by size: S (Morocco/USA/Spain/Germany tops, Italy + Brazil track pants), XS (Argentina top, Portugal polo + track pants, Italy polo), XL (France polo, Italy polo, Argentina shorts). **Not one size M is purchasable across all 44 products.** No bottoms in M, no tops in M, nothing in M. Sizing down to the Italy S with the chart in front of me? Waist an inch too small on a tapered track pant — no.
**Expected:** To settle for *something* — I came to spend £27 and would have stretched to £30.
**Felt:** "I'm not fussy, I'm a medium. A MEDIUM. The most common size there is, and this whole shop has zero of them. There's nothing to settle FOR."

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Didn't — the target product is 0/10 (all in-hand AND all pre-order variants sold out), no notify mechanism exists, and there was nothing to settle for: not a single size-M variant is purchasable anywhere in the 44-product store. Never reached checkout; there was nothing to take there.
**Total time:** ~10 minutes — most of it hunting for a restock signal the store doesn't have.
**Worst moment:** Tapping "M - PRE ORDER" — the mechanism that exists specifically to take money for unmade stock — and finding even that struck through, with no notify-me fallback, while a popup begged for my email for "discounts" and a purple Buy-with-shop button glowed under the dead Sold Out button.
**Best moment:** The Shop all grid being straight with me — SOLD OUT badges on the cards before I tapped, "+" quick-add only on genuinely buyable items. The store never wasted a click getting me TO the bad news; it just had nothing for me once I arrived.
**Would they come back?** Only via TikTok, where she now follows the brand — the store itself captured nothing: no notify-me, no restock info, and a newsletter pitched at "releases and promotions" she signed up to in spirit but was never offered in the one form she wanted ("tell me when M is back"). If the algorithm goes quiet, she's gone.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** A back-in-stock notify button on the dead variant — this shopper arrived with £27 ready, hunted for five minutes for ANY way to leave her email against the M, and the store's answer was a generic popup, an About-page line about "limited releases", and a 2009-style contact form. (Failing that: restock the most common size in the catalogue — zero Ms across 44 products is a stock plan, not bad luck.)
