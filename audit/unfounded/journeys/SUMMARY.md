**14 of 20 reached the checkout door.** Every true abandonment (01, 03, 06,

As with CROOKSLDN, nobody left over how the site looks.

## The five worst moments

1. **Zero size-M variants purchasable across all 44 products** (06) — a
   size-M shopper literally cannot give the store money, and no notify
   mechanism exists to catch them on the way out. The audit's starkest
   commercial fact.
2. **The sold-out PDP's dead-but-lit buttons** (01, 06, 11): greyed "Sold
   Out" above a fully-coloured "Buy with shop" and "MORE PAYMENT OPTIONS"
   that silently do nothing — plus **"Only 0 left in stock. Order soon."**
   "Teaches you to distrust every button on the site" (11).
3. **Shipping cost is unknowable before surrendering an address** (every
   buying persona): the policy page 404s, no banner, no cart line — an £18
   impulse and a £94 basket both reached the door blind (07, 08).
4. **The sale machinery that isn't** (05, 07): empty "Black Friday" and
   "End Of Month Sale" collections live in August, SALE collections with
   zero markdowns, duplicate listings £50/£40 — "the SALE labels lowered
   trust rather than raised it."
5. **The newsletter popup** (07, 13, 14, 15, 16, 18): fires ~10-15s in on
   whatever you're doing — over search results being tapped, over the buy
   box, stacked on the open cart drawer; no accessible name, ignores Escape,
   68 Tab presses to dismiss with Subscribe one stop before NO THANKS.

## Appeared in three or more journeys

**Negative:** no notify-me anywhere (01, 03, 06, 09, 11, 19) · shipping cost
invisible (all buyers) · "Available in 5 size" cards overselling dead
variants (03, 06, 07, 08, 14) · descriptions with zero product facts (02,
03, 10, 16, 19) · warehouse jargon "In Hand / Pre order" leaking into sizes,
cart and checkout (02, 08, 13, 16, 18) · the popup (six journeys) · terms
checkbox silently blocking Checkout (04, 05, 10, 13, 15, 17) · typos on the
trust path ("truely unique", "merchants terms") (01, 14, 15) · size chart
buried as a gallery photo (02, 03, 07, 17).

**Positive:** instant add-to-cart drawer with correct maths (04, 07, 08, 13,
14, 16, 19) · penny-perfect totals at the door (05, 08, 13, 19) · branded
checkout (02, 04, 08, 13, 19) · honest SOLD OUT badges pre-click (06, 07,
11) · the in-stock filter (11, 19) · genuinely-written returns policy with a
real address (02, 03, 09, 12, 20) · Companies House/VAT legitimacy (01, 02)
· strong photography on the flagship line (01, 10, 19).
