**Did:** Realised the track pants should be S, not XS (they run small per the size chart). Looked all over the cart line for a size editor.
**Got:** Nothing — the line offers quantity, bin, and the product name as a link back to the PDP. No "edit size", no variant dropdown. So: opened the PDP, tapped S, added — cart now had TWO Portugal Track Pants lines (XS and S), £101.00, badge 4 — then binned the XS line to land on £71.00, badge 3. The maths held through every intermediate state, but for a moment I owned two pairs of the same trousers and it was on me to notice.
**Expected:** An "edit" on the cart line, like most big stores — one tap, size changed.
**Felt:** "A size swap is three taps and a danger zone here. If I'd got distracted mid-swap I'd have paid £30 for trousers that don't fit AND £30 for the ones that do."

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Bought (audit sense) — reached the checkout landing page with a 4-item, £94.00 basket (Italy Polo XS x2 £46 + Portugal Track Pants S £30 + Argentina Shorts XL £18) that matched the cart to the penny after 3 adds, 1 change of mind, 1 removal, a manual size swap and 2 quantity edits.
**Total time:** ~14 minutes — maybe 4 of them doing the store's job (re-checking real sizes behind "Available in 5 size" cards, and hand-rolling a size swap).
**Worst moment:** The Argentina Shorts stepper showing "2" while the line total said £18 and the red message admitted only 1 was added — the one moment the cart's numbers disagreed with each other, which for a maths-watcher poisons trust in every other number. Close second: no in-cart size editing, so a simple XS→S swap meant a two-lines-of-the-same-trousers danger state.
**Best moment:** The running maths everywhere else — nine subtotal states (£23, £48, £78, £53, £71, £101, £71, £94 and back from the capped bump) all correct in real time, drawer and badge never out of step, and a checkout door reading exactly £94.00. Honourable mention: a cross-sell that only suggests things you can actually buy.
**Would they come back?** Yes for the cart itself — it's the most competent part of the site. But he'd arrive braced: no delivery cost until you surrender an address, no undo on removals, and collection cards whose size claims he's learned not to believe.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** An "edit size" control on each cart line (variant dropdown or a one-tap edit sheet). Dre's basket survived because he was vigilant; the swap-by-hand detour plus the lying stepper are exactly where a less careful five-a-side captain ends up with two XS track pants, or none — and a free-shipping threshold line would likely have grown this £94 basket even further.
