### Step 5 — Returns policy: 1 tap, and it's the best-written page on the site
**Did:** Footer → Returns Policy (1 tap from the homepage).
**Got:** A genuinely thorough policy: 14 days from delivery to tell them, a further 14 to send it back, item conditions. "How to start a return: To start a return, please contact us at **info@unfoundedstudios.com**. Please include: Your order number / Your full name / The item you would like to return / The reason for the return." Then, once approved, post it to **Unfounded Studios Returns, 11 Oxted Green, Milford, Surrey, GU8 5DA**. Catches: "Customers are responsible for return postage costs unless the item is faulty", "We recommend using a tracked delivery service", refunds within 14 days of receiving the item back.
**Expected:** A template, or a portal demanding a login.
**Felt:** "This I can actually do, signed out, today: one email with my order number and name — no account, no form, no portal. Old-fashioned, and the approval step means waiting on a human before I can even post it, but it's honest and complete. Postage is on me though — call it £3-4 of my £25 gone."
**Taps to return instructions: 1.** **Completable signed out with just an order number: yes** (order no. + name + item + reason, by email).
**Next:** continued

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Didn't — not a buying trip. Tracking: found NO on-site route; the only order-status link a Shopify store issues lives in her confirmation email, and the site never once tells her that (zero mentions of dispatch, confirmation emails or parcel tracking anywhere). Return: found a complete, signed-out-friendly process in 1 tap and left ready to send the email from her own inbox.
**Total time:** ~16 minutes — roughly 10 of them spent proving to herself that order tracking genuinely doesn't exist on the site.
**Worst moment:** Searching "track order" and being shown eleven pairs of track pants, then three guessed URLs all landing on "Oops!" — the site has literally no vocabulary for a customer who has already paid.
**Best moment:** The returns policy — 14+14 days, a named email, a real Surrey address, honest caveats, refund timelines — the one page on the site written for a post-purchase human, reachable in one tap and completable signed out.
**Would they come back?** To the site, as a customer needing service — no, and that's the finding: everything post-purchase routes to her inbox (tracking link) or an email thread (returns). As a shopper — maybe; the returns page reads trustworthy, but "no exchanges" against near-empty shelves means a wrong size is a refund and a goodbye, and she'd size up nervously next time.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** One line, anywhere — footer or FAQ: "Where's my order? Use the tracking link in your confirmation email, or sign in with the email you ordered with." The account door (email + one-time code) already works for guest checkouts; the site just never tells anyone.
