# 02 — Dan, 34, wary first-timer who's never heard of this label
**Device:** iPhone-class mobile 390x844, normal network · **Goal:** a small label wants £35 for a hoodie — find contact, returns, shipping cost and proof anyone shops here BEFORE trusting them with a card number · **Mood:** interested but suspicious; one bad smell and he's gone

### Step 1 — Landed on the homepage and got a popup before anything else
**Did:** Opened unfoundedstudios.com on the phone. Within a couple of seconds a newsletter overlay appeared: "NEWSLETTER — Sign up to find out about future releases and promotions. EMAIL / Subscribe". Tapped "NO THANKS".
**Got:** Behind it: black header with a bird logo, hero image of two lads in football polos, "World Cup Drop Live! / All Nation Items Available Now!", then "Our latest collections" — three of three collection tiles flagged SALE ("Black + Grey Items 6", "Nation Polo Sets 10", "Nation Items 28").
**Expected:** A homepage that tells me who these people are. Instead it's straight to merch.
**Felt:** "Asking for my email before I know your surname — classic. And everything's on SALE in August? 'All Nation Items Available Now!' — we'll see about that."
**Next:** continued — scrolled the full page looking for trust signals
**Screens:** u02-01-landing, u02-02-home-top

### Step 2 — Scrolled to the footer hunting for the boring-but-vital links
**Did:** Scrolled the whole homepage to the footer. No reviews, no press logos, no "as seen in" anywhere on the way down.
**Got:** Footer nav is thin: Search / About us / Privacy Policy / Returns Policy — that's it. No Contact link, no Shipping, no FAQ, no Terms. But underneath: "Copyright © 2026 Unfounded Limited All rights reserved. **VAT No. GB 514170427**", payment method icons, and unlabeled Instagram + TikTok icons.
**Expected:** Contact and Delivery/Shipping links in the footer — every real shop has them.
**Felt:** "A VAT number is the first genuinely reassuring thing I've seen — scammers don't usually bother. But no contact link and no shipping info in the footer is a poor look."
**Next:** continued — opened the burger menu
**Screens:** u02-04-footer

### Step 3 — Burger menu: there IS a Contact page (2 taps), footer never mentions it
**Did:** Tapped the burger (tap 1). Menu: Home / Shop all / Collections / **Contact**, with Account, a UK region picker and IG/TikTok icons at the bottom. Tapped Contact (tap 2).
**Got:** A proper contact page: "Opening hours — Monday to Friday 9am to 9pm (GMT), Saturday 10am to 6pm, Sunday 10am to 4pm", a form (first/last name, "HOW DO YOU WANT US TO CONTACT YOU?" email/phone/SMS, email required, message, Send Message) and an embedded map at the bottom. I did NOT send anything. Oddly the page shows no actual email address or phone number — form or nothing.
**Expected:** An email address I can write down in case the form goes into a void.
**Felt:** "Opening hours seven days a week and a map — that's more than most small labels manage. But give me the address in writing, not just a widget."
**Taps to contact: 2** (email address itself surfaces elsewhere — footer → About us, 1 tap, ends with "Get in touch: info@unfoundedstudios.com").
**Next:** continued
**Screens:** u02-03-menu-open, u02-08-contact-probe, u02-05-about

### Step 4 — Returns policy: 1 tap, and it reads like a real operation
**Did:** Footer → "Returns Policy" (1 tap from homepage).
**Got:** A thorough, plain-English policy: "You have 14 days from the date your order is delivered to let us know", a further 14 days to send back, item conditions, and — the clincher — a real return address: "Unfounded Studios Returns, 11 Oxted Green, Milford, Surrey, GU8 5DA, United Kingdom". Also the catches: "Customers are responsible for return postage costs unless the item is faulty" and "We do not currently offer automatic exchanges" (return + re-order for a size swap).
**Expected:** Either a copy-pasted template or nothing.
**Felt:** "This is written by someone who has actually processed a return. A street address in Surrey settles me more than any badge would. Return postage on me and no exchanges — stingy for a label this size, but honest."
**Taps to returns: 1.**
**Next:** continued
**Screens:** u02-06-returns

### Step 5 — Shipping cost: the trail goes cold — the policy page is a 404
**Did:** No shipping link anywhere, so I guessed the standard URL /policies/shipping-policy.
**Got:** "**Oops! We can't find what you're looking for here.**" — the shipping policy page does not exist. The privacy policy (menu-adjacent, footer) is Shopify GDPR boilerplate "Last updated: April 23, 2026" naming a data controller address "Little Nutcombe, Portsmouth Road, Hindhead, ENG, GU26 6AQ, GB" — a second real address, fine, but nothing about delivery prices there either.
**Expected:** "UK shipping £3.99, free over £50" — one sentence, anywhere.
**Felt:** "A clothing shop with no shipping page in 2026. So the price I'm judging isn't the real price — £35 plus mystery postage. That's the kind of thing that gets abandoned baskets."
**Next:** continued — suspicious, but not done yet
**Screens:** u02-09-shipping-policy, u02-07-privacy

### Step 6 — Social proof: zero on site, but the socials and Companies House are real
**Did:** Zero reviews, stars or press anywhere on the site. Tapped the footer Instagram icon (1 tap — instagram.com/unfoundedstudios, opens in a new tab); TikTok icon alongside (@unfoundedstudios). Then did what any sceptic does: googled them.
**Got:** Active TikTok presence with drop announcements ("DROP JUNE 22ND #ukstreetwear") and third-party "Unfounded Studios review" videos; Instagram profile "UnfoundedStudios - Clothing Brand". Companies House: **UNFOUNDED LIMITED, company no. 16490854, active, incorporated 2 June 2025**, registered for internet retail at 71-75 Shelton Street, London WC2H 9JQ.
**Expected:** Honestly, I expected the trail to go dead.
**Felt:** "Incorporated a year ago, VAT-registered, live socials with real kids wearing the gear, review videos by people who aren't the brand. It's a young company but it's a *company*. The site itself shows none of this — I had to leave to find it."
**Taps to social proof: 1** (footer IG/TikTok icon; on-site reviews: nonexistent).
**Next:** continued — decision time
**Screens:** u02-12 not captured (Instagram blocks the audit browser; link verified real)

### Step 7 — The £35 hoodie I actually came for is a wall of "sold out"
**Did:** Opened the Bird Hoodie - Grey PDP (£35).
**Got:** "SOLD OUT" price flag, and every one of ten variants — "XS - IN HAND" through "XL - PRE ORDER" — reads "VARIANT SOLD OUT OR UNAVAILABLE". The button is a dead "Sold Out". No notify-me. Confusing stockroom jargon everywhere: sizes are named "XS - IN HAND" / "XS - PRE ORDER" with two shipping promises ("In Hand Please Allow 5-7 Working Days", "Pre Order - Please Allow 3-4 Weeks"). Below: badges "FAST GLOBAL SHIPPING / 100% SECURE PAYMENTS" — claims, no numbers.
**Expected:** The homepage said "All Nation Items Available Now!" — I expected stock.
**Felt:** "The thing I came to buy doesn't exist to buy, and 'In Hand' vs 'Pre Order' as part of the SIZE name is warehouse-speak leaking onto the shelf. At least tell me when it's back."
**Next:** hesitated — then decided to test the till with something in stock

### Step 8 — Took an in-stock Italy Polo (£23) to the checkout door
**Did:** From the homepage, tapped the Italy Polo card (tap 1). 3 photos — flat product, a size-chart *photo* (pit-to-pit table in inches, buried as gallery image 2), a lifestyle shot. Tapped "M - IN HAND" (tap 2) — crossed out, tap does nothing; only XS is actually buyable ("Only 9 left in stock. Order soon." says the counter, while four of five sizes are dead). Added XS to cart (tap 3). Drawer slid in instantly: ITALY POLO, £23.00, "XS - In Hand", Subtotal £23.00 GBP, "Taxes included." Ticked the mandatory gate "**Agree to terms of sale as per the merchants terms of service.**" (tap 4 — missing apostrophe, and the terms it points to aren't linked anywhere in the footer). Tapped Checkout (tap 5).

Shipping: "**Enter your shipping address to view available shipping methods.**" — the cost stays hidden unless I hand over my address first. STOPPED here, entered nothing.
**Expected:** A delivery price before the checkout asks who I am.
**Felt:** "The till itself is legit — proper Shopify, big-brand payment buttons, nothing home-rolled asking for my card. But I got to the checkout door and I *still* don't know what postage costs. And I tapped M and the cart quietly held XS."

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** "Bought" — reached the checkout landing page with the Italy Polo £23 (XS, the only live size) and genuine intent; the £35 Bird hoodie he actually came for was 100% sold out with no notify-me.
**Total time:** ~22 minutes (a third of it off-site, verifying the company because the site itself offers no proof).
**Worst moment:** Discovering the shipping policy page is a 404 and the checkout still wouldn't name a delivery price without his address — the exact number a sceptic needs is the one number the site never shows.
**Best moment:** The returns policy — 14+14 days, a real Surrey return address, honest caveats — plus the VAT number and an active Companies House record. The paperwork of a real business.
**Would they come back?** Maybe. Trust: cautiously yes — this is a young but genuine registered company with a safe Shopify checkout, and he'd have risked £35 through Google Pay. But he'd only actually return if the hoodie restocks, and he'd grumble about postage-roulette the whole way.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** A shipping cost stated anywhere before the address form — one line in the footer or on the PDP. (Close second: any customer reviews on-site; every scrap of social proof lives off-site.)
