# 03 — Dan, size-anxious buyer (waist ~33", between M and L, burned before by bad fits)
**Device:** iPhone-class mobile 390x844, normal network · **Goal:** the £30 Italy Track Pants — but only if he can work out which size won't need returning · **Mood:** wary; he's done the "guess the size, pay to send it back" dance twice before and won't do it again

### Step 1 — Landed on the homepage
**Did:** Opened unfoundedstudios.com on his phone. A newsletter popup appeared over the page; tapped "NO THANKS" (tap 1). Scrolled the homepage.
**Got:** "World Cup Drop Live!" hero, then a "Latest arrivals" rail that includes ITALY TRACK PANTS £30 right there on the homepage. The card says "Available in 5 size" with chips reading "XS - IN HAND / S - IN HAND / M - IN HAND / +2".
**Expected:** To have to dig through a menu; instead the product was one scroll away. But "Available in 5 size" plus an "IN HAND" badge on M reads like *my size is in stock*.
**Felt:** "Alright, easy. Five sizes, M's 'in hand', whatever that means. Promising."
**Next:** continued — tapped the card (tap 2).
**Screens:** u03-01-arrival, u03-02-menu

### Step 2 — PDP: the size row punches him in the face
**Did:** Landed on /products/italy-track-pants. Read the buy block, tapped through the sizes — tapped "M - IN HAND" directly.
**Got:** Five size pills labelled "XS - IN HAND", "S - IN HAND"… — but XS, M, L and XL are all struck through "VARIANT SOLD OUT OR UNAVAILABLE". Only S is selectable. Tapping M does nothing; selection snaps back to S. Below the buttons: "Only 3 left in stock. Order soon." No notify-me, no restock date, nothing on the dead sizes. The entire product description is one line: "Please Allow 2-5 Working Days For Item To Be Shipped".
**Expected:** The homepage card said five sizes and stamped "IN HAND" on M. Turns out "In Hand" is just part of the variant *name*, and 4 of 5 sizes are dead. The scarcity timer ("Only 3 left!") is nagging him to hurry up and buy a size that isn't his.
**Felt:** "'M - IN HAND' and 'SOLD OUT' on the same button. Which is it? And the description is a shipping disclaimer — not one word about the actual trousers."
**Next:** hesitated — started hunting for sizing info anyway, in case S runs big or a restock is worth waiting for.
**Screens:** u03-12-size-row, u03-13-tapped-M, u03-03-pdp-top

### Step 3 — The sizing hunt (timed)
**Did:** Hunted for any sizing help, ~4 minutes: read the one-line description (nothing), scrolled the whole PDP for a "Size guide" link or accordion (none), opened the menu — Home / Shop all / Collections / Contact, no size guide — checked the footer (Search / About us / Privacy / Returns — no size guide), then went back and swiped the photo gallery: product flat-lay, shorts mode (they zip into shorts — first time the page tells him that, via photo only), pants mode, a lifestyle shot… and then photo 5 of 5: **a size chart**, hiding as the last gallery image. Tapped it — opens a full-screen lightbox, perfectly legible: INCH — Waist 28/30/32/34/36, Length 39.5–43.5, Leg Opening 8–10 for XS–XL. Later probes confirmed there is no size-guide page anywhere (/pages/size-guide, /pages/sizing, /pages/size-chart, /pages/faq all 404; site search for "size chart" returns 2 unrelated products).
**Got:** Real numbers at last: M = 32" waist, L = 34". Dan is a 33 — dead between the two, and there's no "model wears", no fit advice, no cm row, nothing to break the tie. The chart itself looks plausibly real — garment measurements with sane half-inch steps, shared across the track-pant colourways — though the perfectly uniform +2/+1/+0.5 progression smells like a supplier template rather than someone measuring these actual trousers.
**Expected:** A "Size guide" link next to the size picker. Not a chart buried as the fifth photo with a thumbnail the size of a postage stamp, half cut off at the screen edge.
**Felt:** "There IS a chart — they just hid it in the photo reel. If I hadn't swiped to the end out of stubbornness I'd have sworn this site had nothing. And after all that: I'm a 33, they made a 32 and a 34, and both of those are sold out anyway."
**Next:** continued — checked the sibling track pants before giving up.
**Screens:** u03-04-pdp-mid, u03-05-pdp-bottom, u03-06-chart-slide, u03-11-search-size

### Step 4 — Any track pants in his size at all?
**Did:** Tapped Portugal Track Pants (£30) from "You may also like"; also checked Brazil Track Pants.
**Got:** Portugal: XS and S available, M/L/XL sold out. Brazil: S only. Same "Only 3 left in stock" urgency line on Portugal too. Across every track-pant colourway on the site there is **no M and no L in stock anywhere** — the only sizes left are the ones nobody his size can wear.
**Expected:** A rival colourway to bail him out.
**Felt:** "The whole wall's smalls. This isn't a shop with my size in the back — this is the end of a clearance rail."
**Next:** went back — one last check: what happens if he gambles on a size and it's wrong?
**Screens:** u03-07-portugal-pdp

### Step 5 — Returns policy: who pays for a wrong size?
**Did:** From the PDP, scrolled past the recommendations to the footer and tapped "Returns Policy" (1 tap from the PDP; 3 taps total from landing). Read it in full. (Note for the record: the tap-target exists twice in the page markup and our script hit a hidden duplicate; a human tapping the visible footer link gets there — one tap either way.)
**Got:** A genuinely thorough, human-written policy: 14 days to notify + 14 further days to send back; items unworn with tags; email info@unfoundedstudios.com with order number first; return address in Milford, Surrey. The two lines that matter to Dan: **"Customers are responsible for return postage costs unless the item is faulty"** and **"We do not currently offer automatic exchanges. If you would like a different size… return your original order for a refund and place a new order."**
**Expected:** Exactly this, honestly — but hoped for free size exchanges like the big shops do.
**Felt:** "So if I guess wrong I pay the postage back, wait up to 14 days for my refund, and re-order from scratch — assuming my size ever exists again. That's the exact trap I came here to avoid. At least they're straight about it."
**Next:** gave up — nothing left to decide.
**Screens:** u03-08-pdp-footer, u03-09-returns-policy, u03-10-returns-mid

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Didn't buy — bailed at the PDP. His size is M or L; the chart (once found) says M=32" / L=34" for his 33" waist, and both are sold out in every track-pant colourway on the site. Only S (30" waist) is purchasable — three inches too small, with no exchange offered and return postage on him if he gambles. He never touched the cart.
**Total time:** ~11 minutes (about 4 of them hunting for sizing info).
**Worst moment:** Tapping "M - IN HAND" and having it say "VARIANT SOLD OUT OR UNAVAILABLE" in the same breath — after the homepage card had waved "Available in 5 size" and "M - IN HAND" chips at him. The store's own labels told him his size was in stock twice before the PDP told him the truth.
**Best moment:** Swiping to photo 5 of 5 and finding an actual, legible size chart in a full-screen lightbox — real waist/length/leg-opening measurements, better data than plenty of bigger stores give. It's the one piece of sizing help on the entire site, and it's unlabelled, unlinked, and pure luck to find.
**Would they come back?** Only if he happened to hear the M or L came back — and he won't hear, because there's no back-in-stock notify, no restock date, and no waitlist. Realistically: no.
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** His size existing. Failing that, a notify-me on the dead sizes would have kept him — he was willing to wait; the site just gave him no way to.
