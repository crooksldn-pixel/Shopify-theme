# 01 — Cold Instagram click, first-time visitor who tapped a story link for the grey Bird hoodie
**Device:** iPhone-class mobile 390x844, slow 4G, on the bus, one hand · **Goal:** saw the hoodie in an Instagram story — what is this, what does it cost, do they do my size (L), is this shop real, when would it arrive · **Mood:** mildly curious, zero brand knowledge, ~90 seconds of patience

*(Session note: the audit egress IP geo-detects as US, so the first hit rendered "$49.00"; the session was re-pinned with `?country=GB` to match this UK shopper, giving £35.00. A real UK visitor sees GBP throughout — everything below is the GBP view. USD arrival kept as u01-01 for the record.)*

### Step 1 — Tapped the story link and waited
**Did:** Opened /products/bird-grey-hoodie straight from Instagram on slow 4G.
**Got:** ~9–10 seconds of blank/partial page before it settled. Then a genuinely clean PDP: black header with a bird logo, big flat-lay photo of the hoodie, a row of 5 thumbnails (front, back, two proper lifestyle shots, another colour), "SOLD OUT" badge, "Bird Hoodie - Grey", "£35.00 · TAXES INCLUDED", and dispatch lines: "In Hand Please Allow 5-7 Working Days From Purchase / Pre Order - Please Allow 3-4 Weeks For Your Item".
**Expected:** The hoodie from the ad, a price, and a buy button within a couple of seconds.
**Felt:** "Slow… okay, there it is. £35, taxes in — fair. Looks like the ad. Hold on — SOLD OUT badge already?"
**Next:** continued (u01-01, u01-02)

### Step 2 — Newsletter popup landed mid-read
**Did:** Was reading the price and shipping lines about 15 seconds in when a "Sign up for our newsletter — Stay informed about new collections and discounts" sheet slid over the bottom half of the screen and dimmed the whole page. It followed me while scrolling until dealt with.
**Got:** Email field + Subscribe + a small underlined "NO THANKS". Tapped NO THANKS, it closed cleanly. (Read-only rule: nothing was entered or submitted.)
**Expected:** To be left alone for at least the first visit — I've known this brand for 20 seconds.
**Felt:** "You want my email before I even know if you have my size? No thanks — literally."
**Next:** continued, slightly irritated (u01-03, u01-04, u01-05, u01-08)

### Step 3 — Looked for size L
**Did:** Scrolled to SIZE and scanned the chips, then tapped "L - IN HAND" anyway.
**Got:** TEN chips — XS to XL "IN HAND" and XS to XL "PRE ORDER" — every single one struck through. The tap did nothing (selection stayed on the default "XS - IN HAND", buy button stayed a greyed "Sold Out"). Verified via the product data: 0 of 10 variants purchasable — even all five *pre-order* slots are sold out. No "notify me when back", no waitlist, nothing. Confusingly, a bright purple active-looking "Buy with shop" button still sits right under the dead Sold Out button.
**Expected:** Either an L I can buy, a pre-order date, or an "email me when it's back" button.
**Felt:** "Everything's gone — even the pre-orders? Why are you running ads for this? And the purple button looks buyable, which is just confusing. So the popup wants my email but the one place I'd actually give it — 'tell me when L is back' — doesn't exist."
**Next:** hesitated (u01-04, u01-09)

### Step 4 — Sanity-checked the product page for details and trust
**Did:** Swiped the gallery, scrolled the rest of the page looking for fabric/fit info, reviews, and any sign this shop is real.
**Got:** 11 gallery images — genuinely good: flat-lays front/back, lifestyle shots on a London-looking estate. But zero product description: no fabric, no weight, no fit note, no size guide, no measurements — the only body copy on the whole PDP is the two dispatch lines. No reviews or ratings anywhere. Trust strip says "FAST GLOBAL SHIPPING / 100% SECURE PAYMENTS" (no numbers). Footer: Unfounded Limited, VAT No. GB 514170427, payment icons, Returns Policy and About us links — but no contact page anywhere in the footer, and shipping *cost* appears nowhere.
**Expected:** A sentence about the hoodie (gsm, fit), a size chart, maybe some reviews.
**Felt:** "Photos say real brand, VAT number says real company — fine, probably not a scam. But you're telling me nothing about the actual hoodie, and I'd be guessing on fit even if L existed."
**Next:** continued (u01-05, u01-12, u01-07)

### Step 5 — Tried the one thing that looked buyable
**Did:** In "You may also like", the Italy Polo (£23) showed "Choose Options / Available in 5 size" — tapped it. ~5s load.
**Got:** A football polo, not a hoodie. £23, "Please Allow 2-5 Working Days". Size chips: XS pre-selected, S/M/L struck through, only XS and XL actually in stock — so "Available in 5 size" on the card was a lie, and my L is dead here too. It does have what the hoodie didn't: a size chart as a gallery image (pit-to-pit/length/sleeve in inches). Below the buttons: "Only 9 left in stock. Order soon."
**Expected:** The card said 5 sizes — I expected 5 sizes.
**Felt:** "Second product, second dead end in my size. 'Available in 5 size', 'Only 9 left' — the urgency lines feel like they're for a shop that has stock. This one isn't for me anyway."
**Next:** went back (u01-13, u01-14, u01-15)

### Step 6 — One last glance at who these people are, then out
**Did:** Tapped the bird logo to see the homepage while deciding.
**Got:** Slick full-bleed football editorial, hero banners "World Cup Drop Live!" and "All Nation Items Available Now!", collections all prefixed "SALE", strapline "Clothing designs to make everyone feel truely unique" (typo and all).
**Expected:** A quick read on the brand — got it: football-culture streetwear, decent shoots.
**Felt:** "'All Nation Items Available Now!' — mate, I've just hit two sold-out walls. Looks good, but there's nothing here for me today." Bus stop's coming.
**Next:** gave up — closed the tab (u01-17, u01-18)

## Outcome
**Bought / didn't:** Didn't — never got past a dead buy button; the product they came for has 0 of 10 variants available (all in-hand AND all pre-order sold out).
**Total time:** ~4 minutes.
**Worst moment:** Realising every size including all pre-orders was struck through, with no notify-me anywhere — while a newsletter popup had just begged for an email for "new collections and discounts", and an active-looking purple "Buy with shop" button sat under the dead Sold Out button.
**Best moment:** The gallery — 11 real photos including proper lifestyle shots — plus a visible VAT number; in ten seconds the shop went from "random Instagram ad" to "real brand".
**Would they come back?** Only if the algorithm reshows them; they left no email (popup ignored, and there was no back-in-stock capture to leave one on), bookmarked nothing, and the parting impression was "nice brand that doesn't have anything to sell me".
**One thing that would have changed the outcome:** A "Notify me when L is back" button on the sold-out PDP — this shopper actively wanted to hand over their email for exactly that and was never offered it (failing that: don't run/leave story ads pointing at a 0/10-stock product).
