# COMPARISON AUDIT — THE TEN PERSONAS

The eight personas from the CROOKSLDN audit (re-verified in run 2), plus two built for
cross-site comparison. Each persona runs identically on all seven sites (six competitors +
the crooks theme), on the same devices and throttles as the crooks audit: mobile 390×844,
Slow 4G, 4× CPU unless stated. The scripted spine is collected by `c-journeys.mjs`; the
felt-experience notes are authored per site from that evidence.

| # | Persona | Entry point | What they test | Key measures |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Cold social click** | Straight onto a PDP, throttled mobile | Does the first viewport answer what/cost/size/available before patience runs out? | LCP, CLS, overlays at arrival, first-viewport answers, ATC visibility |
| 2 | **Returning fan** | Homepage, knows the brand | Can they tell what's new since last visit? | New-arrival signals: dates, badges, "just dropped" copy |
| 3 | **Size-anxious buyer** | PDP, between sizes, burned before | Will this fit? Can they verify before paying? | Size guide presence, measurement quality (units, dimensions, laid-flat method, model info) |
| 4 | **The sceptic** | Homepage, deciding if this is a real business | Trust plumbing: policies, contact, identity | Policy pages complete (no placeholders), contact details, address, email consistency |
| 5 | **Aimless browser** | Homepage, no intent | Is there a reason to stay and return? | Editorial/lookbook/playful hooks, newsletter pitch, social links |
| 6 | **Accessibility user** | Home + PDP, keyboard and AT | Can they complete the purchase path? | axe violations, tab order, focus visibility, ATC reachable by keyboard, 200% zoom reflow |
| 7 | **Slow connection** | Homepage, older phone profile | What loads first, and when is the page usable? | Wall-clock to load, LCP, product image in first viewport, page weight |
| 8 | **Post-purchase** | Homepage, already bought | Can they track, return, get help? | Track/returns/account/FAQ links findable from the homepage |
| 9 | **Comparison shopper** *(new)* | PDP, six tabs open, deciding where £60 goes | The cross-tab decision spine: what does this tab answer that others don't? | Price clarity, shipping cost stated, returns cost stated, material stated, made-where |
| 10 | **Gift buyer** *(new)* | Homepage, buying for someone else, novice | Can a stranger to the brand buy safely for a third party? | Gift cards, size help from home, exchange policy visibility, return window visibility, category clarity |

## Why 9 and 10 were added for the comparison

**The comparison shopper** is the audit's own frame made into a persona: six competitor tabs
open, one decision. Sites win this persona not by being good in isolation but by *answering
decisive questions the other tabs leave open* — delivery cost, returns cost, fabric weight.
This is where CROOKSLDN's honesty positioning either cashes out or doesn't.

**The gift buyer** is the hardest test of navigation and policy clarity: no brand knowledge, no
fit knowledge, spending on someone else's behalf. Streetwear brands lose December revenue here.
They punish in-fiction labels hardest — which makes them the fairest stress test of the crooks
voice against conventional competitors.
