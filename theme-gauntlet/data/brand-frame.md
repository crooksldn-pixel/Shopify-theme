## Positioning implied by the site itself (council's reading grid)

- positioning: a founder-led online art school where meditative drawing is the
  vehicle for wellbeing and manifestation; authority anchored on Anna Denning,
  co-creation with Colette Baron-Reid, and a Hay House book.
- adjectives the copy reaches for (perception targets to test in cold recall):
  calming, transformative, spiritual, creative, nurturing, credible, premium
  (premium applies to the certification tier specifically).
- target customer: adults (skewing women 30-65) seeking calm, mindfulness and
  self-development through accessible art practice; a professional tier
  (coaches/teachers/therapists) for certification.
- price position: mixed ladder - $59-$99 consumer courses, $28-38/mo
  membership, £997-£1497 professional certification.

## What the council tests

Did cold buyers' five-second reads, adjectives, price guesses, and
tell-a-friend sentences land inside this frame, outside it, or against it -
and which page moments (with shots) did the landing or the missing.
